# Appendix D: Sample Financial Statements

## Overview

This appendix provides complete sample financial statements for different Luxembourg legal entity forms. These examples follow PCN 2020 requirements and Luxembourg accounting standards.

## Contents

1. Sample Financial Statements for SA (Société Anonyme / Corporation)
2. Sample Financial Statements for SARL (Société à Responsabilité Limitée / LLC)
3. Sample Financial Statements for SNC (Société en Nom Collectif / General Partnership)
4. Notes to Financial Statements Examples

## Important Notes

- These are template examples for educational purposes
- Actual financial statements must be prepared according to your specific circumstances
- Consult with a qualified accountant or auditor for your actual financial statements
- All amounts are in Euros (€)
- Statements follow PCN 2020 format
- Income tax lines are simplified round figures for illustration, not computed at statutory rates (in Luxembourg City the combined CIT + solidarity surcharge + municipal business tax rate is 24.94 % for 2024 and 23.87 % from 2025)

## 4. Notes to Financial Statements (Annexe)

### Standard Notes Structure

**Note 1: Accounting Policies**
- Basis of preparation
- Accounting standards applied (PCN 2020)
- Currency (EUR)
- Fiscal year

**Note 2: Fixed Assets**
- Breakdown by category
- Depreciation methods and rates
- Additions and disposals during year

**Note 3: Inventory**
- Valuation method (FIFO, weighted average)
- Breakdown by category

**Note 4: Trade Receivables**
- Aging analysis
- Provisions for bad debts

**Note 5: Trade Payables**
- Terms and conditions
- Aging analysis

**Note 6: VAT**
- VAT payable
- VAT recoverable
- Net position

**Note 7: Personnel**
- Number of employees
- Social charges breakdown

**Note 8: Related Party Transactions**
- Transactions with related parties
- Terms and conditions

**Note 9: Commitments and Contingencies**
- Leases
- Guarantees
- Legal proceedings

**Note 10: Subsequent Events**
- Events after balance sheet date

## Abbreviated vs. Full Financial Statements

### Abbreviated Statements (Abregé)
- Available for smaller companies
- Simplified format
- Fewer notes required
- Criteria (small company, 2 of 3 limits not exceeded for two consecutive years): balance sheet total ≤ €7.5M, net turnover ≤ €15M, average staff ≤ 50

### Full Statements (Complet)
- Required for larger companies
- Detailed format
- Comprehensive notes
- More disclosure requirements

## Filing Requirements

### Electronic Filing
- All financial statements must be filed electronically
- Balance sheet and profit and loss account prepared in structured form on eCDF, then filed with the RCS via the LBR portal
- Notes and any management/audit reports attached as PDF

### Language Requirements
- Can be in French, German, or English
- Must be consistent across all documents

## Important Reminders

- Financial statements must be prepared in accordance with PCN 2020
- Must be filed within 7 months of fiscal year end
- Must be signed by company representatives
- Auditor's report required for certain companies
- Keep copies for 10 years (document retention requirement)
