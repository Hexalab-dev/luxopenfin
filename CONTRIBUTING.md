# Contributing to Luxembourg SME Accounting Textbook

Thank you for your interest in contributing to this open educational resource!

## Contribution Guidelines

### Content Standards

- All content must be accurate and verified against official Luxembourg regulations
- Use clear, accessible language appropriate for SME owners and accounting students
- Include practical examples from Luxembourg business contexts
- Maintain consistency with OpenStax pedagogical standards

### Review Process

- All submissions will be peer-reviewed by Luxembourg accounting professionals
- Content must be verified against current Luxembourg regulations (PCN 2020, VAT rules, etc.)
- Technical accuracy is paramount - cite official sources

### Language Requirements

- Primary languages: French, German, English
- Maintain parallel content across all three languages
- Use consistent terminology (see Glossary in Appendix F)

### Format Requirements

- Markdown format for text content
- Follow chapter template structure
- Include all required pedagogical elements (learning objectives, key terms, exercises, etc.)

## How to Contribute

1. Review the existing outline in `lux.plan.md`
2. Identify chapters or sections you'd like to contribute
3. Follow the chapter template structure
4. Submit for peer review
5. Incorporate feedback and revisions

## Areas Needing Contribution

- Sector-specific case studies
- Real-world examples from Luxembourg SMEs
- Software integration guides
- Video tutorials
- Translation assistance
- Technical review

## Questions?

Please open an issue with your questions or suggestions.
