## Überblick

This appendix provides a comprehensive calendar of important tax and accounting deadlines for Luxembourg SMEs. All dates are critical for compliance and avoiding penalties.

## Annual Calendar

### January

**15th - VAT Return (Quarterly and Monthly)**
- Q4 VAT return due (previous year, quarterly filers)
- December VAT return due (monthly filers)
- Zahlung fällig, wenn TVA zu zahlen

**31st - Annual Accounts Filing**
- For companies with fiscal year ending June 30
- File annual accounts to RCS

### February

**15th - VAT Return (Monthly)**
- January VAT return due (if monthly filer)

**28th/29th - VAT Return (Annual)**
- Annual VAT return due before 1 March (previous year)
- For businesses on annual-only filing (turnover up to €112,000)

**Corporate Tax Advances (IRC/ICC, Net Wealth Tax)**
- Quarterly advance payments are due on the dates stated in the tax administration's (ACD) advance-payment notice (for IRC/ICC typically 10 March, 10 June, 10 September and 10 December)

### March

**15th - VAT Return (Monthly)**
- February VAT return due (if monthly filer)

**31st - Annual Accounts Filing**
- For companies with fiscal year ending August 31

### April

**15th - VAT Return (Quarterly and Monthly)**
- Q1 VAT return due (quarterly filers)
- March VAT return due (monthly filers)
- Zahlung fällig, wenn TVA zu zahlen

**30th - VAT Return (Annual, periodic filers)**
- Annual VAT return due before 1 May (previous year) for monthly and quarterly filers

**30th - Annual Accounts Approval Planning**
- Convene the general meeting: annual accounts must be approved within 6 months of year-end (by June 30 for a December 31 year-end)

### May

**15th - VAT Return (Monthly)**
- April VAT return due (if monthly filer)

**31st - Annual Accounts Filing**
- For companies with fiscal year ending October 31

### June

**15th - VAT Return (Monthly)**
- May VAT return due (if monthly filer)

**30th - Annual Accounts Approval / Mid-Year Review**
- Deadline for shareholders to approve the annual accounts (December 31 year-end: within 6 months)
- Review of corporate tax advance payments; ask the ACD to adjust them if necessary

### July

**15th - VAT Return (Quarterly and Monthly)**
- Q2 VAT return due (quarterly filers)
- June VAT return due (monthly filers)
- Zahlung fällig, wenn TVA zu zahlen

**31st - Annual Accounts Filing (Most Common)**
- **CRITICAL DEADLINE**: For companies with fiscal year ending December 31
- Annual accounts must be filed within 7 months of fiscal year end (approval within 6 months, filing within 1 month of approval)
- This is the most common deadline for Luxembourg companies

### August

**15th - VAT Return (Monthly)**
- July VAT return due (if monthly filer)

**31st - Annual Accounts Filing**
- For companies with fiscal year ending January 31

### September

**15th - VAT Return (Monthly)**
- August VAT return due (if monthly filer)

**Corporate Tax Advance (IRC/ICC)**
- Quarterly advance per the ACD notice (typically 10 September)

### October

**15th - VAT Return (Quarterly and Monthly)**
- Q3 VAT return due (quarterly filers)
- September VAT return due (monthly filers)
- Zahlung fällig, wenn TVA zu zahlen

**31st - Annual Accounts Filing**
- For companies with fiscal year ending March 31

### November

**15th - VAT Return (Monthly)**
- October VAT return due (if monthly filer)

**Corporate Tax Return Preparation**
- Finalise the previous year's corporate tax return (due December 31)

### December

**15th - VAT Return (Monthly)**
- November VAT return due (if monthly filer)

**31st - Corporate Tax Return Deadline**
- Corporate tax return (modèle 500: IRC, ICC and net wealth tax) for the previous year due, filed electronically via MyGuichet.lu
- Quarterly IRC/ICC advance per the ACD notice (typically 10 December)

**31st - Fiscal Year End**
- Most common fiscal year end date
- Begin preparation for annual accounts

## Filing Deadlines by Document Type

### Annual Accounts (Comptes Annuels)

**Standard Deadline:**
- **7 months** after fiscal year end
- Most companies: July 31 (for December 31 year-end)

**Filing Anforderungen:**
- Balance Sheet (Bilan)
- Income Statement (Compte de Résultat)
- Notes to Financial Statements (Annexe)
- Balance sheet and P&L prepared on eCDF, then filed with the RCS via the LBR portal (notes attached as PDF)

**Late Filing Penalties:**
- Higher filing fees for late filing
- Under the LBR compliance procedure (2026): formal request, then daily penalties and ultimately striking-off from the register for persistent non-filing

### VAT Returns (Déclarations TVA)

**Monthly Filers (Umsatz über 620.000 €):**
- Due: 15th of following month
- Payment: Same date if VAT payable

**Quarterly Filers (Umsatz 112.000 € – 620.000 €):**
- Due: 15th of month following quarter end
- Q1: 15. April
- Q2: 15. Juli
- Q3: 15. Oktober
- Q4: 15. Januar (folgendes Jahr)

**Annual Filers (Umsatz bis 112.000 €):**
- Due: vor dem 1. März (folgendes Jahr)
- Monatliche und vierteljährliche Einreicher reichen zusätzlich eine Jahreserklärung vor dem 1. Mai (folgendes Jahr) ein
- Die AED legt die Einreichungsregelung schriftlich fest

**Late Filing Penalties:**
- €25 - €250 per month of delay
- Interest on late payments

### Corporate Income Tax (Impôt sur le Revenu des Collectivités)

**Filing Deadline:**
- December 31 of the year following the tax year (one return, modèle 500, covers IRC, municipal business tax and net wealth tax)
- Filed electronically via MyGuichet.lu
- Extra time may be granted on justified request

**Payment Deadlines:**
- Quarterly advances on the dates in the ACD advance-payment notice (IRC/ICC typically 10 March, 10 June, 10 September, 10 December)
- Balance: after the assessment, generally by the end of the month following its receipt

**Late Filing Penalties:**
- Late-filing surcharges and late-payment interest may apply

### Municipal Business Tax (Impôt Commercial Communal)

**Filing Deadline:**
- Same corporate tax return (modèle 500) and same deadline as IRC
- Rate depends on the municipality (6.75% in Luxembourg City)

**Payment:**
- Quarterly advances with the IRC advances; balance after assessment

### Payroll Tax and Social Charges

**Monthly Filers:**
- Due: 15th of following month
- Umfasst: Social security, health insurance, pension contributions

**Quarterly Filers:**
- Due: 15th of month following quarter end
- For smaller employers

**Annual Reconciliation:**
- Due: February 28 (following year)
- Reconcile all payroll taxes and social charges

## Important Dates for SMEs

### Start of Year (January)
- Review previous year's performance
- Plan for upcoming year
- Set up new fiscal year in accounting system
- Review and update chart of accounts if needed

### Mid-Year (June/July)
- Review first half performance
- Adjust budgets and forecasts
- Review tax estimates
- Prepare for annual accounts (if December year-end)

### Year-End (December)
- Close accounting period
- Perform inventory count (if applicable)
- Reconcile all accounts
- Begin annual accounts preparation
- Review and update internal controls

## Compliance Checklist by Month

### Every Month
- [ ] Record all transactions
- [ ] Reconcile bank accounts
- [ ] Review accounts receivable and payable
- [ ] File VAT return (if monthly filer)
- [ ] Pay payroll taxes and social charges

### Every Quarter
- [ ] File quarterly VAT return
- [ ] Review financial statements
- [ ] Reconcile all balance sheet accounts
- [ ] Review budget vs. actual
- [ ] Update cash flow forecasts

### Annually
- [ ] Prepare annual accounts
- [ ] File annual accounts with RCS
- [ ] File corporate tax return (modèle 500: IRC, municipal business tax, net wealth tax) by December 31 of the following year
- [ ] Annual payroll reconciliation
- [ ] Review and update accounting policies
- [ ] Archive documents (10-year retention requirement)
- [ ] Prepare FAIA file (if required)

## Special Deadlines

### New Business Registration
- **Within 3 months** of starting business activity
- Register with RCS
- Register for VAT (if applicable)
- Register for social security

### Business Closure
- **Within 1 month** of ceasing activity
- File final tax returns
- Close VAT registration
- File final annual accounts

### Change in Business Structure
- **Within 1 month** of change
- Update RCS registration
- Update VAT registration if needed
- Notify tax authorities

## Reminders and Best Practices

### Setting Up Reminders
- Use calendar alerts for all deadlines
- Set reminders 1 week before each deadline
- Create recurring reminders for monthly/quarterly tasks

### Documentation
- Keep copies of all filed returns
- Maintain organized records
- Document any extensions or special arrangements

### Professional Help
- Consult with accountant or tax advisor
- Review deadlines annually
- Stay informed about regulatory changes

## Hinweise

- All dates are based on standard deadlines
- Deadlines may vary for specific circumstances
- Always verify current deadlines with official sources
- Extensions may be available with proper justification
- This calendar is for guidance only; official regulations take precedence
- Dates falling on weekends/holidays are typically extended to next business day
