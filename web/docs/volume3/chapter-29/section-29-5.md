# 29.5 Payroll Accounting in PCN

### Understanding Payroll Accounting

Payroll accounting in Luxembourg must follow PCN (Plan Comptable Normalisé) standards. All payroll transactions must be recorded using appropriate PCN accounts to ensure compliance and accurate financial reporting.

### PCN Accounts for Payroll

#### Salary Expense Accounts

**Account 6211: Gross Salaries (Salaires bruts)**
- Records gross salaries paid to employees (base salaries 62111, bonuses 62114, benefits in kind 62115)
- Debit: When salaries are paid
- Includes all salary components (base, bonuses, overtime)

**Account 623: Social Security Charges (Employer Portion)**
- Records employer social charge expenses (6231 pension contributions, 6232 other social security contributions)
- Debit: When employer social charges are paid
- Includes all employer contributions

#### Social Charge Liability Accounts

**Account 4621: Social Security Payable – CCSS (Centre commun de la sécurité sociale)**
- Records social charges owed (employee + employer)
- Credit: When social charges are calculated
- Debit: When social charges are paid
- Balance: Shows unpaid social charges

Wage tax withheld from salaries (retenue d'impôt sur traitements et salaires) is credited to Account 46124 and net salaries owed to staff to Account 4714.

#### Cash/Bank Accounts

**Account 5131: Bank Account**
- Records salary payments
- Credit: When salaries and social charges are paid from bank

**Account 516: Cash on Hand**
- Records cash salary payments (if applicable)
- Rarely used for payroll in modern businesses

### Payroll Journal Entries

#### Entry 1: Calculate and Record Payroll

**Transaction**: Pay employee €3,500 gross salary, employee social charges €453.25, employer social charges €439.95 (2026 rates, see Section 29.4)

**PCN Accounting:**
- **Debit**: Account 6211 (Gross Salaries) - €3,500.00
- **Debit**: Account 623 (Employer Social Charges) - €439.95
- **Credit**: Account 4621 (Social Charges Payable – CCSS) - €893.20
- **Credit**: Account 5131 (Bank) - €3,046.75

**Explanation:**
- Debit 6211: Record gross salary expense
- Debit 623: Record employer social charge expense
- Credit 4621: Record total social charges payable (employee + employer)
- Credit 5131: Pay net salary to employee

#### Entry 2: Pay Social Charges

**Transaction**: Pay social charges to CCSS

**PCN Accounting:**
- **Debit**: Account 4621 (Social Charges Payable – CCSS) - €893.20
- **Credit**: Account 5131 (Bank) - €893.20

**Explanation:**
- Debit 4621: Reduce social charges payable
- Credit 5131: Pay social charges from bank

### Monthly Payroll Process

#### Step 1: Calculate Payroll

- Calculate gross salaries for all employees
- Calculate employee social charges
- Calculate employer social charges
- Calculate net salaries

#### Step 2: Record Payroll

- Journal entry for each employee (or batch entry)
- Record gross salary expense
- Record employer social charge expense
- Record social charges payable
- Record net salary payment

#### Step 3: Pay Employees

- Transfer net salaries to employee bank accounts
- Update bank account records

#### Step 4: Pay Social Charges

- Pay social charges to CCSS
- Record payment in accounting system

### Accrual Accounting for Payroll

#### Month-End Accruals

**If Payroll Paid in Following Month:**

**Month-End Entry (Accrue Payroll):**
- **Debit**: Account 6211 (Gross Salaries) - €3,500.00
- **Debit**: Account 623 (Employer Social Charges) - €439.95
- **Credit**: Account 4621 (Social Charges Payable – CCSS) - €893.20
- **Credit**: Account 4714 (Salaries Payable – amounts owed to staff) - €3,046.75

**Following Month (Pay Payroll):**
- **Debit**: Account 4714 (Salaries Payable – amounts owed to staff) - €3,046.75
- **Credit**: Account 5131 (Bank) - €3,046.75

### Sub-Accounts for Detail

#### Employee-Specific Accounts

**Structure:**
- 621111: Salaries - Employee 1 (sub-account of 62111)
- 621112: Salaries - Employee 2
- 621113: Salaries - Employee 3

**Benefits:**
- Detailed tracking by employee
- Easier reconciliation
- Better reporting

#### Department-Specific Accounts

**Structure:**
- 621111: Salaries - Production (alternative structure, sub-account of 62111)
- 621112: Salaries - Sales
- 621113: Salaries - Administration

**Benefits:**
- Cost allocation by department
- Departmental reporting
- Budget analysis

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

**Important Requirements:**
- **PCN compliance**: Use correct PCN accounts
- **Accurate recording**: Record all payroll transactions accurately
- **Timely recording**: Record in correct accounting period
- **Reconciliation**: Reconcile payroll accounts regularly
- **Documentation**: Maintain supporting documentation

**Common Issues:**
- **Wrong accounts**: Using incorrect PCN accounts
- **Timing errors**: Recording in wrong period
- **Calculation errors**: Incorrect amounts recorded
- **Missing entries**: Not recording all payroll transactions

### Concepts in Practice

**Complete Payroll Accounting**

*TechLux Solutions* monthly payroll:

**Employees:**
- 5 employees, total gross: €20,000
- Total employee social charges: €2,590 (12.95%)
- Total employer social charges: €2,514 (12.57%, Mutualité class 1)

**Journal Entry:**
- **Debit**: Account 6211 (Gross Salaries) - €20,000.00
- **Debit**: Account 623 (Employer Social Charges) - €2,514.00
- **Credit**: Account 4621 (Social Charges Payable – CCSS) - €5,104.00
- **Credit**: Account 5131 (Bank) - €17,410.00

**Payment Entry:**
- **Debit**: Account 4621 (Social Charges Payable – CCSS) - €5,104.00
- **Credit**: Account 5131 (Bank) - €5,104.00

**Total Payroll Cost**: €22,514.00
