# Chapter 29 – Exercises & Cases

## Multiple Choice Questions

1. Total employee social charges in Luxembourg are:
   a) 12%
   b) 12.95%
   c) 16%
   d) 18%
   **Answer:** b) Total employee social charges are 12.95% of gross salary (2.80% + 0.25% health, 8.50% pension, 1.40% dependency; rates as of 1 January 2026).

2. The contribution ceiling for social charges is:
   a) €9,000 per month
   b) €10,000 per month
   c) €13,518.70 per month
   d) €15,000 per month
   **Answer:** c) The contribution ceiling is 5 × the social minimum wage = €13,518.70 per month (from 1 January 2026).

3. Employer social charges range from:
   a) 10% - 11%
   b) 12.57% - 15.00%
   c) 15.7% - 16.7%
   d) 17% - 18%
   **Answer:** b) Employer social charges range from 12.57% - 15.00% depending on the Mutualité des employeurs class (rates as of 1 January 2026).

4. Meal vouchers are exempt from social charges up to:
   a) €8.00 per day
   b) €10.80 per day
   c) €12.00 per day
   d) €15.00 per day
   **Answer:** b) Meal vouchers are exempt up to €10.80 per day (as of 2025).

5. The minimum wage for skilled workers is:
   a) €2,500 per month
   b) €2,703.74 per month
   c) €3,000 per month
   d) €3,244.49 per month
   **Answer:** d) The minimum wage for skilled workers is €3,244.49 per month (120% of the €2,703.74 unskilled social minimum wage, from 1 January 2026).

6. Payroll declarations must be filed by:
   a) 10th of following month
   b) 15th of following month
   c) 20th of following month
   d) End of following month
   **Answer:** b) Payroll declarations must be filed by 15th of following month.

7. Account 6211 in PCN is used for:
   a) Employer social charges
   b) Salaries and wages
   c) Social charges payable
   d) Bank account
   **Answer:** b) Account 6211 (Salaires bruts) is used for gross salaries and wages. Employer social charges go to 623, social charges payable to 4621 and the bank to 5131.

8. Overtime pay for hours beyond the first 2 hours is:
   a) 125% of regular rate
   b) 150% of regular rate
   c) 175% of regular rate
   d) 200% of regular rate
   **Answer:** d) Overtime pay for hours beyond the first 2 hours is 200% of regular rate.

9. Standard vacation entitlement is:
   a) 20 working days
   b) 25 working days
   c) 30 working days
   d) 35 working days
   **Answer:** b) Standard vacation entitlement is 25 working days per year.

10. Luxembourg has how many public holidays?
    a) 9
    b) 10
    c) 11
    d) 12
    **Answer:** c) Luxembourg has 11 public holidays.
