# References and Further Reading

## Official Luxembourg Sources

- **CCSS (Centre Commun de la Sécurité Sociale)**: https://www.ccss.lu – Social security administration, payroll guidance
- **Ministry of Labor**: https://www.gouvernement.lu – Labor law, minimum wage, employment regulations
- **eCDF Platform**: https://www.ecdf.lu – Electronic payroll declaration filing
- **Luxembourg Labor Code**: Complete labor legislation

## Payroll Guidance and Resources

- **CCSS Payroll Guides**: Official guides on payroll calculation and reporting
- **Minimum Wage Information**: Official minimum wage rates and updates
- **Social Charge Calculators**: Online tools for calculating social charges
- **Payroll Software**: Approved payroll software providers

## Professional Organizations

- **Ordre des Experts-Comptables (OEC)**: https://www.oec.lu – Professional accounting body, payroll training
- **House of Entrepreneurship**: https://www.cc.lu/en/ho – Business support, payroll guidance for SMEs
- **Chamber of Commerce**: https://www.cc.lu – Business resources, employment information

## Labor Law Resources

- **Labor Inspectorate**: Official labor law enforcement and guidance
- **Employment Contracts**: Templates and guidance for employment contracts
- **Labor Law Updates**: Regular updates on labor law changes

## Payroll Software and Services

- **Payroll Software Providers**: List of approved payroll software
- **Payroll Service Providers**: Professional payroll services for SMEs
- **Integration Guides**: Guides for integrating payroll with accounting systems

## Online Resources

- **CCSS Online Services**: https://www.ccss.lu – Online payroll services and information
- **Luxembourg Business Portal**: https://www.guichet.public.lu – Business registration and employment information
- **Payroll Calculators**: Online tools for payroll calculations

## Training and Education

- **OEC Payroll Training**: Professional payroll training programs
- **House of Entrepreneurship Workshops**: SME payroll workshops
- **Online Payroll Courses**: Web-based payroll training
- **CCSS Seminars**: Social security authority payroll seminars
