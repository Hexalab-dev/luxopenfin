# 29.8 Luxembourg Minimum Wage and Labor Law Considerations

### Understanding Minimum Wage

Luxembourg has a **guaranteed minimum wage** that applies to all employees. Employers must ensure that all employees receive at least the minimum wage, adjusted for age and qualifications.

### Minimum Wage Rates

#### Standard Minimum Wage

**Adult Minimum Wage (social minimum wage, from 1 January 2026):**
- **€2,703.74 per month** (for unskilled workers aged 18+)
- **€3,244.49 per month** (for skilled workers, +20%)
- Adjusted through wage indexation and periodic legal revaluations; check the current amounts each year

#### Age-Adjusted Minimum Wage

**Youth Minimum Wage:**
- **17-18 years**: 80% of adult minimum wage
- **15-17 years**: 75% of adult minimum wage

**Example:**
- Adult minimum wage: €2,703.74
- 17-year-old minimum wage: €2,703.74 × 80% = €2,162.99

### Skilled vs. Unskilled Workers

#### Skilled Worker Definition

**Skilled Worker:**
- Has completed recognized vocational training
- Has professional qualification
- Has minimum 2 years of experience in field
- Meets other qualification criteria

**Skilled Worker Minimum Wage:**
- 20% higher than unskilled minimum wage
- **€3,244.49 per month** (from 1 January 2026)

#### Unskilled Worker

**Unskilled Worker:**
- Does not meet skilled worker criteria
- **€2,703.74 per month** (from 1 January 2026)

### Working Hours and Overtime

#### Standard Working Hours

**Standard Workweek:**
- **40 hours per week** (standard)
- **8 hours per day** (standard)
- Maximum 10 hours per day (with conditions)
- Maximum 48 hours per week (with conditions)

#### Overtime Rules

**Overtime Pay:**
- **First 2 hours**: 150% of regular rate
- **After 2 hours**: 200% of regular rate
- **Sunday/holiday work**: 200% of regular rate
- **Night work**: Additional premiums may apply

### Vacation and Holidays

#### Paid Vacation

**Vacation Entitlement:**
- **25 working days per year** (minimum)
- Additional days for seniority (up to 2 additional days)
- Vacation pay: Regular salary during vacation
- Must be taken within calendar year (with exceptions)

#### Public Holidays

**Public Holidays:**
- Luxembourg has **11 public holidays**
- Employees entitled to paid public holidays
- If worked, double pay typically applies
- Public holidays: New Year, Easter Monday, Labor Day, Ascension, Whit Monday, National Day, Assumption, All Saints, Christmas, Boxing Day, plus one variable

### Employment Contracts

#### Written Contracts

**Contract Requirements:**
- Written employment contract required
- Must include: Salary, working hours, job description, start date
- Must comply with labor law requirements
- Must be provided within specified time

#### Contract Types

**Types:**
- **Permanent contract (CDI)**: Indefinite term
- **Fixed-term contract (CDD)**: Specific duration
- **Part-time contract**: Reduced hours
- **Temporary contract**: For specific projects

### Termination and Notice Periods

#### Notice Periods

**Notice Requirements:**
- Minimum notice periods based on service length
- **0-5 years**: 2 months notice
- **5-10 years**: 4 months notice
- **10+ years**: 6 months notice

#### Termination Protection

**Protection:**
- Unfair dismissal protection
- Required procedures for termination
- Potential compensation for unfair dismissal

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

**Critical Requirements:**
- **Minimum wage**: Must pay at least minimum wage
- **Working hours**: Comply with working hour limits
- **Overtime**: Pay correct overtime rates
- **Vacation**: Provide minimum vacation entitlement
- **Public holidays**: Comply with public holiday requirements
- **Contracts**: Provide written employment contracts
- **Termination**: Follow proper termination procedures

**Common Issues:**
- **Below minimum wage**: Paying below minimum wage
- **Overtime violations**: Not paying correct overtime rates
- **Vacation violations**: Not providing required vacation
- **Contract issues**: Not providing proper contracts
- **Termination issues**: Not following proper procedures

- Qualification: High school diploma only
- **Minimum wage**: €2,703.74/month (unskilled)
- **Actual salary**: €2,750/month (above minimum) ✅

- Age: 17
- Qualification: Student
- **Minimum wage**: €2,703.74 × 80% = €2,162.99/month

**Compliance**: All employees paid above minimum wage, all labor law requirements met.
