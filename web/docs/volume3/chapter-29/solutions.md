# Chapter 29 – Solutions

## Multiple Choice Solutions

1. **b)** Total employee social charges are 12.95% of gross salary (2.80% + 0.25% health, 8.50% pension, 1.40% dependency; rates as of 1 January 2026).

2. **c)** The contribution ceiling is 5 × the social minimum wage = €13,518.70 per month (from 1 January 2026).

3. **b)** Employer social charges range from 12.57% - 15.00% depending on the Mutualité des employeurs class (rates as of 1 January 2026).

4. **b)** Meal vouchers are exempt up to €10.80 per day (as of 2025).

5. **d)** The minimum wage for skilled workers is €3,244.49 per month (120% of the €2,703.74 unskilled social minimum wage, from 1 January 2026).

6. **b)** Payroll declarations must be filed by 15th of following month.

7. **b)** Account 6211 is used for gross salaries and wages.

8. **d)** Overtime pay for hours beyond the first 2 hours is 200% of regular rate.

9. **b)** Standard vacation entitlement is 25 working days per year.

10. **c)** Luxembourg has 11 public holidays.
