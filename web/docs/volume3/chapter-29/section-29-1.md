# 29.1 Luxembourg Social Security System Overview

### Understanding the Social Security System

Luxembourg's **social security system** is one of the most comprehensive in Europe, providing extensive coverage for health insurance, pensions, unemployment, family benefits, and other social protections. Both employers and employees contribute to the system through social charges.

### Social Security Components

:::note Rates as of 1 January 2026
All rates in this chapter are the CCSS rates applicable from **1 January 2026**. They change regularly (for example, pension was 8% per party until 31 December 2025), so check **ccss.lu** each year before using them.
:::

#### 1. Health Insurance (Assurance Maladie)

**Coverage:**
- Medical care and hospitalization
- Prescription drugs
- Dental and vision care
- Preventive care

**Contributions:**
- Employee contribution: 2.80% (benefits in kind) + 0.25% (cash benefits) = 3.05% of gross salary
- Employer contribution: 2.80% + 0.25% = 3.05% of gross salary
- **Total**: 6.10% of gross salary

#### 2. Pension Insurance (Assurance Pension)

**Coverage:**
- Retirement pensions
- Disability pensions
- Survivor benefits

**Contributions:**
- Employee contribution: 8.50% of gross salary
- Employer contribution: 8.50% of gross salary
- **Total**: 17% of gross salary

#### 3. Dependency Insurance (Assurance Dépendance)

**Coverage:**
- Long-term care benefits
- Dependency support

**Contributions:**
- **Employee only**: 1.40%, computed on gross salary minus 1/4 of the social minimum wage (no ceiling)
- No employer contribution

#### 4. Accident Insurance (Assurance Accident)

**Coverage:**
- Work-related accidents
- Occupational diseases

**Contributions:**
- **Employer only**: 0.65% of gross salary, multiplied by a bonus-malus factor (0.85–1.50) based on the employer's accident record

#### 5. Health at Work (Santé au travail, STM)

- **Employer only**: 0.14% of gross salary

#### 6. Mutualité des Employeurs

**Coverage:**
- Reimburses employers for salary continued during employee sick leave

**Contributions:**
- **Employer only**: 0.23% / 0.95% / 1.56% / 2.66% (classes 1–4, by the employer's absenteeism rate)

**Note:** There is no separate "employment fund" payroll contribution. The Fonds pour l'emploi is financed through the solidarity surcharge on income tax, not through social security deductions.

### Total Social Charges

#### Employee Contributions

**Total Employee Social Charges:**
- Health Insurance: 2.80% + 0.25%
- Pension Insurance: 8.50%
- Dependency Insurance: 1.40%
- **Total Employee**: 12.95% of gross salary (approximation: applies 1.40% to full gross, ignoring the 1/4 social minimum wage allowance)

#### Employer Contributions

**Total Employer Social Charges:**
- Health Insurance: 2.80% + 0.25%
- Pension Insurance: 8.50%
- Accident Insurance: 0.65%
- Health at Work: 0.14%
- Mutualité des employeurs: 0.23% – 2.66%
- **Total Employer**: about 12.57% (Mutualité class 1) to 15.00% (class 4) of gross salary

#### Combined Total

**Total Social Charges:**
- Employee: 12.95%
- Employer: 12.57% – 15.00%
- **Combined**: about 25.5% – 28.0% of gross salary

*Worked examples in this chapter assume Mutualité class 1 (0.23%), i.e. an employer rate of 12.57%.*

### Social Security Administration

#### CCSS (Centre Commun de la Sécurité Sociale)

**Responsibilities:**
- Administers social security system
- Collects contributions
- Manages benefits
- Provides information and support

**Reporting:**
- Monthly payroll declarations required
- Electronic filing through eCDF or CCSS portal
- Payment of contributions required

### Salary Caps

#### Contribution Ceilings

**Maximum Salary Subject to Contributions:**
- Ceiling: 5 × the social minimum wage = 5 × €2,703.74 = €13,518.70 per month (from 1 January 2026)
- Applies to health, pension, accident, health-at-work and Mutualité contributions
- Dependency insurance has **no** ceiling

**Important**: Capped contributions are calculated only on salary up to the ceiling

**Example:**
- Gross salary: €15,000 per month
- Contribution base for pension/health: €13,518.70 (capped)
- Dependency insurance still calculated on the full salary (minus 1/4 of the social minimum wage)

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

**Important Requirements:**
- **Mandatory participation**: All employees must be enrolled
- **Monthly reporting**: Payroll declarations required monthly
- **Timely payment**: Contributions must be paid on time
- **Accurate calculation**: Errors result in penalties
- **Record keeping**: Maintain payroll records for 10 years

**Common Issues:**
- **Calculation errors**: Incorrect social charge calculations
- **Late payments**: Missing payment deadlines
- **Incorrect reporting**: Errors in payroll declarations
- **Missing employees**: Not enrolling all eligible employees

### Concepts in Practice

**Social Charges Calculation**

*Artisan Boulangerie* employee payroll:

**Gross Salary**: €3,500 per month

**Employee Contributions (12.95%):**
- Health Insurance (2.80% + 0.25%): €98.00 + €8.75 = €106.75
- Pension Insurance (8.50%): €297.50
- Dependency Insurance (1.40%): €49.00 *(simplified: applied to full gross; strictly (€3,500 − €675.94) × 1.40% = €39.54)*
- **Total Employee**: €453.25

**Employer Contributions (12.57%, Mutualité class 1):**
- Health Insurance (2.80% + 0.25%): €106.75
- Pension Insurance (8.50%): €297.50
- Accident Insurance (0.65%): €22.75
- Health at Work (0.14%): €4.90
- Mutualité des employeurs (0.23%): €8.05
- **Total Employer**: €439.95

**Total Social Charges**: €453.25 + €439.95 = €893.20

**Employee Net Salary (before income tax withholding)**: €3,500 - €453.25 = €3,046.75

**Total Labor Cost**: €3,500 + €439.95 = €3,939.95
