# Key Terms

- **Point-of-Sale (POS) System**: System for recording sales transactions at point of sale
- **Cetrel**: Luxembourg payment system for card payments
- **Tip**: Gratuity given to service staff (cash tips vs. card tips/service charges)
- **FIFO (First-In-First-Out)**: Inventory valuation method assuming oldest inventory sold first
- **Weighted Average**: Inventory valuation method using average cost
- **Customer Returns**: Products returned by customers, reducing sales
- **Customer Allowances**: Price reductions for damaged goods or discounts
- **Time-Based Billing**: Billing method based on hours worked
- **Project Accounting**: Accounting system tracking costs and revenue by project
- **Revenue Recognition**: Process of recording revenue when earned
- **Advance Payments (Unearned Revenue)**: Payments received before service delivery
- **Retainer**: Prepaid service agreement, recognized as services provided
- **Payment Gateway**: Service processing online payments
- **Cross-Border VAT**: VAT rules for sales across EU borders
- **Digital Goods**: Electronic products (software, e-books, music, videos)
- **One-Stop Shop (OSS)**: EU system for simplified VAT reporting
- **Direct Materials**: Raw materials directly used in production
- **Direct Labor**: Labor directly related to production
- **Job Costing**: Cost accounting method tracking costs by job/order
- **Custom Order**: Unique product order for specific customer
