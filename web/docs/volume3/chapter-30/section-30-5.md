# 30.5 eCDF Platform: Electronic Submission

## Overview

The **eCDF (électronique Collecte des Données Financières)** platform is Luxembourg's mandatory electronic system for submitting financial data. The balance sheet and profit and loss account are prepared and validated on eCDF in the standard structured format; the annual accounts are then filed with the RCS through the LBR portal, where the structured data are pulled from eCDF and the notes and any reports are attached as PDF.

## eCDF Platform Features

### Electronic Filing System

**eCDF** provides:
- Secure electronic filing of annual accounts
- Integration with RCS (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés)
- Digital signature capabilities
- Document management
- Filing history and tracking

### Mandatory Electronic Filing

**Article 30** of the Accounting Law requires **electronic filing** of annual accounts through the eCDF platform.

**Exceptions**: Very limited exceptions may apply in specific circumstances, but electronic filing is the standard requirement.

## eCDF Registration and Access

### Registration Requirements

**To use eCDF, companies must:**
1. Register with eCDF platform
2. Obtain digital certificates for authentication
3. Set up authorized users
4. Complete registration process

### Access Methods

**Access Options:**
- **Direct access**: Through eCDF portal with digital certificate
- **Authorized representative**: Through accountant or legal advisor
- **Software integration**: Through accounting software with eCDF integration

## Filing Process Through eCDF

### Step 1: Prepare Documents

**Required Documents:**
- Balance sheet (in required format)
- Income statement (in required format)
- Notes (in required format)
- Audit report (if applicable)
- Management report (if applicable)

### Step 2: Format Documents

**File Formats:**
- **XML format**: Preferred format for structured data
- **PDF format**: For supporting documents
- **Excel format**: For certain supplementary documents

**Format Requirements:**
- Must comply with eCDF technical specifications
- Must be validated before submission
- Must include required metadata

### Step 3: Upload and Validate

**Upload Process:**
1. Log in to eCDF platform
2. Select filing type (annual accounts)
3. Upload documents
4. Validate format and content
5. Review uploaded documents

**Validation:**
- System validates file format
- System validates required fields
- System checks for completeness
- Errors must be corrected before submission

### Step 4: Digital Signature

**Digital Signature:**
- Documents must be digitally signed
- Signature confirms authenticity
- Signature binds the company to the filing

### Step 5: Submit and Confirm

**Submission:**
1. Review all uploaded documents
2. Apply digital signature
3. Submit filing
4. Receive confirmation
5. Save confirmation for records

## eCDF Technical Requirements

### File Format Specifications

**XML Format:**
- Must follow eCDF XML schema
- Must include all required elements
- Must validate against schema

**PDF Format:**
- Must be searchable PDF
- Must include proper metadata
- Must be properly formatted

### Digital Certificate Requirements

**Certificates:**
- Must be issued by recognized certificate authority
- Must be valid and not expired
- Must be properly installed

## Benefits of Electronic Filing

### Efficiency

**Advantages:**
- Faster processing
- Immediate confirmation
- Reduced paperwork
- Automated validation

### Accuracy

**Benefits:**
- Format validation prevents errors
- Required fields enforced
- Consistency checks
- Reduced manual errors

### Accessibility

**Advantages:**
- 24/7 access to platform
- Online filing history
- Easy document retrieval
- Public access to filed accounts

## Luxembourg Compliance Note

**Critical Requirements:**
- **Mandatory electronic filing**: Must file through eCDF
- **Digital signature**: Documents must be digitally signed
- **Format compliance**: Must follow eCDF technical specifications
- **Timely filing**: Must meet 7-month deadline
- **Record keeping**: Keep confirmation of filing

**Common Issues:**
- **Technical problems**: Platform access or upload issues
- **Format errors**: Documents not in required format
- **Certificate issues**: Digital certificate problems
- **Validation failures**: Documents not passing validation
- **Missing documents**: Not including all required documents
