# References and Further Reading

## Official Luxembourg Sources

- **Law of 19 December 2002**: Accounting Law (Loi Comptable) - Primary legal source for financial reporting
- **Commercial Code**: Code de Commerce - Additional requirements for business registration
- **RCS (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés)**: https://www.rcsl.lu - Trade and companies register
- **eCDF Platform**: https://www.ecdf.lu - Electronic filing platform for annual accounts
- **AED (Administration de l'Enregistrement et des Domaines)**: Tax authorities, FAIA requirements

## Financial Reporting Guidance

- **LUX GAAP Guide**: Official guidance on Luxembourg accounting principles
- **Annual Accounts Templates**: Official templates for balance sheet and income statement
- **RCS Filing Guides**: Official guides on filing procedures
- **eCDF User Manual**: Technical documentation for eCDF platform

## Professional Organizations

- **Ordre des Experts-Comptables (OEC)**: https://www.oec.lu - Professional accounting body, financial reporting guidance
- **House of Entrepreneurship**: https://www.cc.lu/en/ho - Business support, financial reporting guidance for SMEs
- **Chamber of Commerce**: https://www.cc.lu - Business resources, compliance information

## Audit and Compliance

- **Institut des Réviseurs d'Entreprises (IRE)**: Professional body for certified auditors
- **Audit Requirements**: Official guidance on audit requirements and procedures
- **FAIA Specifications**: Technical specifications for FAIA file format
- **Document Retention Guidelines**: Official guidance on document retention requirements

## EU Directives and Standards

- **Fourth Directive (78/660/CEE)**: Annual accounts of certain company forms
- **Fair Value Directive (2001/65/CE)**: Valuation rules for annual accounts
- **Modernization Directive (2003/51/CE)**: Modernization of accounting directives
- **Transparency Directive (2006/46/CE)**: Transparency requirements

## Online Resources

- **RCS Online Portal**: https://www.rcsl.lu - Access to filed annual accounts
- **eCDF Online Services**: https://www.ecdf.lu - Electronic filing services
- **Luxembourg Business Portal**: https://www.guichet.public.lu - Business registration and compliance information
- **Financial Reporting Templates**: Official templates and forms

## Training and Education

- **OEC Financial Reporting Training**: Professional training programs
- **House of Entrepreneurship Workshops**: SME financial reporting workshops
- **Online Financial Reporting Courses**: Web-based training
- **RCS Seminars**: Filing procedures and compliance seminars
