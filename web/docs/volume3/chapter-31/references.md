# References and Further Reading

## Official Luxembourg Sources

- **EU Directive 2014/55/EU**: European e-invoicing directive
- **Luxembourg Tax Authorities (AED)**: Fiscal cash register requirements, FAIA specifications
- **eCDF Platform**: https://www.ecdf.lu - Electronic filing platform
- **RCS (Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés)**: Business registration and compliance

## E-Invoicing Standards

- **UBL (Universal Business Language)**: https://www.oasis-open.org - UBL standard documentation
- **Peppol**: https://peppol.eu - Peppol network and standards
- **Peppol BIS**: Business Interoperability Specifications
- **E-Invoicing Guides**: Official guides on e-invoicing implementation

## Fiscal Cash Registers

- **Certified Equipment List**: List of approved fiscal cash registers
- **Fiscal Cash Register Requirements**: Official requirements and specifications
- **Installation Guides**: Guides for installing and configuring fiscal cash registers

## FAIA and SAF-T

- **OECD SAF-T**: OECD Standard Audit File for Tax documentation
- **FAIA Specifications**: Luxembourg FAIA format specifications
- **FAIA Export Guides**: Guides for generating FAIA files

## Software and Integration

- **Sage BOB**: https://www.sage.com - Accounting software with e-invoicing support
- **Odoo**: https://www.odoo.com - Open-source ERP with e-invoicing modules
- **Software Integration Guides**: Guides for integrating digital compliance features

## Professional Organizations

- **Ordre des Experts-Comptables (OEC)**: https://www.oec.lu - Professional accounting body, digital compliance guidance
- **House of Entrepreneurship**: https://www.cc.lu/en/ho - Business support, digital compliance guidance
- **Chamber of Commerce**: https://www.cc.lu - Business resources, compliance information

## Online Resources

- **Luxembourg Business Portal**: https://www.guichet.public.lu - Business registration and compliance information
- **E-Invoicing Resources**: Online resources and tools for e-invoicing
- **Digital Compliance Guides**: Comprehensive guides on digital compliance

## Training and Education

- **OEC Digital Compliance Training**: Professional training programs
- **House of Entrepreneurship Workshops**: SME digital compliance workshops
- **Online Digital Compliance Courses**: Web-based training
- **Software Vendor Training**: Training from software providers
