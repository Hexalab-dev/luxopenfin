# References and Further Reading

## Official Luxembourg Sources

- **Administration de l'Enregistrement, des Domaines et de la TVA (AED)**: https://www.aed.public.lu – Official VAT authority, registration, and guidance
- **eCDF Platform**: https://www.ecdf.lu – Electronic VAT return filing system
- **Luxembourg VAT Law**: Legal framework for VAT in Luxembourg
- **VAT Rate Schedules**: Official schedules of goods/services by VAT rate

## VAT Guidance and Resources

- **AED VAT Guides**: Official guides on VAT registration, filing, and compliance
- **eCDF User Manual**: Complete guide to using eCDF system
- **VAT Rate Finder**: Online tool to find correct VAT rate for goods/services
- **VAT Calculator**: Tools for calculating VAT amounts

## Professional Organizations

- **Ordre des Experts-Comptables (OEC)**: https://www.oec.lu – Professional accounting body, VAT training and guidance
- **House of Entrepreneurship**: https://www.cc.lu/en/ho – Business support, VAT guidance for SMEs
- **Chamber of Commerce**: https://www.cc.lu – Business resources, VAT information

## EU VAT Resources

- **European Commission VAT**: https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs – EU VAT rules and guidance
- **VAT Information Exchange System (VIES)**: Verify EU VAT numbers
- **OSS Portal**: https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu – One-Stop Shop (OSS) and Import One-Stop Shop (IOSS), which replaced MOSS on 1 July 2021

## Academic and Reference Materials

- **Luxembourg Tax Code**: Complete tax legislation including VAT
- **VAT Implementation Guides**: Practical guides for businesses
- **Cross-Border VAT Guides**: Guides for international transactions
- **Sector-Specific VAT Guides**: Guides for restaurants, retail, services

## Online Resources

- **AED Online Services**: https://www.aed.public.lu – Online VAT services and information
- **Luxembourg Business Portal**: https://www.guichet.public.lu – Business registration and VAT information
- **VAT FAQs**: Frequently asked questions about VAT in Luxembourg

## Training and Education

- **OEC VAT Training**: Professional VAT training programs
- **House of Entrepreneurship Workshops**: SME VAT workshops
- **Online VAT Courses**: Web-based VAT training
- **AED Seminars**: Tax authority VAT seminars
