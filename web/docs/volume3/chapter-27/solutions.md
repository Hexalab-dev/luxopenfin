# Chapter 27 – Solutions

## Multiple Choice Solutions

1. **b)** Luxembourg's standard VAT rate is 17%.

2. **a)** Wine served in restaurants is subject to 17% VAT (only certain wines sold in retail are at 14%).

3. **c)** VAT registration is mandatory when annual turnover exceeds €50,000 (since 1 January 2025; €35,000 before).

4. **b)** Account 461411 represents VAT on sales (output VAT).

5. **c)** Account 421611 is used for input VAT recoverable.

6. **b)** eCDF filing is mandatory for all VAT returns.

7. **b)** Intra-EU B2B supplies are zero-rated when customer provides valid EU VAT number.

8. **b)** If input VAT exceeds output VAT, you are entitled to a refund.

9. **b)** SME VAT Scheme is available for businesses with turnover up to €50,000.

10. **d)** Basic foodstuffs are subject to 3% VAT (super-reduced rate).

## Question Solutions

### Question 1

Luxembourg's four-tier VAT rate structure:
- **17% (Standard)**: Most goods and services, beer/spirits, alcoholic drinks served in restaurants, professional services
- **14% (Intermediate)**: Certain wines (up to 13% vol.), certain energy products
- **8% (Reduced)**: Gas, electricity, hairdressing, small repairs
- **3% (Super-Reduced)**: Food, restaurant meals, books, pharmaceuticals, water, passenger transport, accommodation

### Question 2

VAT registration is mandatory when annual taxable turnover exceeds €50,000 (since 2025; €35,000 before). Benefits of voluntary registration include ability to recover input VAT on purchases, professional image, and B2B advantages.

### Question 3

SME VAT Scheme (small-business franchise, art. 57bis LTVA) means no VAT is charged on sales, with simplified accounting and reduced compliance burden, for businesses with turnover up to €50,000. Limitations include the turnover limit, reduced flexibility, and no input VAT recovery.

### Question 4

**Cash Sale with VAT:**
- Debit Account 516 (Cash on hand): €117.00
- Credit Account 7061 (Sales of goods): €100.00
- Credit Account 461411 (Output VAT): €17.00

### Question 5

Output VAT is VAT collected on sales (recorded in Account 461411). Input VAT is VAT paid on purchases (recorded in Account 421611). Net VAT = Output VAT - Input VAT (recorded in Account 461412 when payable, or 421612 when refundable).

### Question 6

eCDF filing process: Log in, select VAT return type, enter output VAT and input VAT data, review and validate, submit electronically, receive confirmation, pay VAT if due.

### Question 7

Intra-EU B2B transactions are zero-rated when customer provides valid EU VAT number. Reverse charge applies to purchases from EU businesses—you account for VAT in Luxembourg and can recover as input VAT.

### Question 8

Restaurant VAT rules: Food (3%), Wine/alcoholic beverages served (17%), Non-alcoholic beverages (3%). (In retail, certain wines up to 13% vol. are at 14%; beer and spirits at 17%.) Each rate must be shown separately on invoices.

### Question 9

VAT transactions recorded in PCN Class 4: Account 421611 (Input VAT), Account 461411 (Output VAT), Account 461412 (VAT Payable to the AED) and 421612 (VAT receivable). Used to track VAT collected, paid, and net amount due.

### Question 10

Common VAT errors: Wrong rates, missing VAT, incorrect input VAT recovery, late filing, calculation errors. Avoid through software, training, professional support, and internal controls.
