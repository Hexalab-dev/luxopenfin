# Chapter 26 – Exercises & Cases

## Multiple Choice Questions

1. The PCN (Plan Comptable Normalisé) is:
   a) Optional for small businesses
   b) Mandatory for all businesses in Luxembourg
   c) Only required for large corporations
   d) A recommendation, not a requirement
   **Answer:** b) The PCN is mandatory for all businesses operating in Luxembourg.

2. How many main account classes does the PCN have?
   a) Five
   b) Six
   c) Seven
   d) Eight
   **Answer:** c) The PCN has seven main account classes (1-7).

3. Which PCN class is used for customer accounts?
   a) Class 1
   b) Class 4
   c) Class 5
   d) Class 7
   **Answer:** b) Class 4 is used for third-party accounts, including customers (Account 4011, in group 40 Trade receivables).

4. Account 7061 in the PCN represents:
   a) Purchases of goods
   b) Sales of goods for resale
   c) Cash account
   d) Supplier accounts
   **Answer:** b) Account 7061 represents sales of goods for resale (Class 7 - Revenue Accounts, group 70 Net turnover).

5. Which account is used for input VAT recoverable?
   a) 461411
   b) 461412
   c) 421611
   d) 421612
   **Answer:** c) Account 421611 is used for input VAT recoverable (TVA en amont). 461411 is output VAT, 461412 VAT payable to the AED and 421612 a VAT credit receivable from the AED.

6. Sub-accounts in the PCN:
   a) Are not allowed
   b) Can be used for detailed tracking while maintaining compliance
   c) Must follow a different numbering system
   d) Are only for large businesses
   **Answer:** b) Sub-accounts can be used for detailed tracking (e.g., 40111, 40112 under 4011) while maintaining PCN compliance.

7. The current PCN standard is:
   a) PCN 2011
   b) PCN 2015
   c) PCN 2020
   d) PCN 2025
   **Answer:** c) PCN 2020 is the current standard.

8. Which class contains fixed assets?
   a) Class 1
   b) Class 2
   c) Class 3
   d) Class 4
   **Answer:** b) Class 2 contains formation expenses and fixed assets (intangible, tangible and financial).

9. When purchasing goods for resale on credit, which accounts are affected?
   a) Debit 6061, Credit 44111
   b) Debit 44111, Credit 6061
   c) Debit 6061, Credit 5131
   d) Debit 7061, Credit 4011
   **Answer:** a) Purchasing goods for resale on credit: Debit 6061 (Purchases of goods for resale), Credit 44111 (Suppliers).

10. PCN compliance is required for:
    a) Only RCS filings
    b) Only tax returns
    c) Financial statements, RCS filings, and tax returns
    d) Only large businesses
    **Answer:** c) PCN compliance is required for financial statements, RCS filings, and tax returns.

## Problems Set A

### Problem 1: Account Classification

Classify the following accounts into their appropriate PCN classes:

- Bank account
- Customer accounts receivable
- Sales revenue
- Equipment (fixed asset)
- Salaries expense
- Inventory
- VAT payable
- Share capital

**Solution Guide:**
- Bank account: Class 5 (Account 5131)
- Customer accounts receivable: Class 4 (Account 4011)
- Sales revenue: Class 7 (Account 7061 for goods, 703 for services)
- Equipment: Class 2 (Account 222)
- Salaries expense: Class 6 (Account 6211)
- Inventory: Class 3 (Account 361 for goods for resale, 321 for finished goods)
- VAT payable: Class 4 (Account 461411 output VAT; 461412 after the VAT return)
- Share capital: Class 1 (Account 101)

### Problem 2: Transaction Mapping

Map the following transactions to PCN accounts:

a) Purchase goods for resale for €1,000 on credit, VAT 17% included
b) Sell goods for €500 cash, VAT 17% included
c) Pay €800 to supplier
d) Receive €1,170 from customer
e) Pay €2,000 salary (gross)

**Solution Guide:**
a) Debit 6061 (€854.70), Debit 421611 (€145.30), Credit 44111 (€1,000)
b) Debit 516 (€500), Credit 7061 (€427.35), Credit 461411 (€72.65)
c) Debit 44111 (€800), Credit 5131 (€800)
d) Debit 5131 (€1,170), Credit 4011 (€1,170)
e) Debit 6211 (€2,000), Credit 5131 (€2,000) [simplified, excluding social security (4621) and wage tax (46124)]

### Problem 3: Custom Chart of Accounts

Design a customized chart of accounts for a restaurant business. Include:
- Customer segmentation (retail, wholesale, online)
- Product categories (food, beverages)
- Expense categories (food purchases, rent, utilities)

**Solution Guide:**
- 40111: Retail customers
- 40112: Wholesale customers
- 40113: Online customers
- 70331: Food sales (meals served)
- 70611: Beverage sales
- 6011: Food purchases
- 6012: Beverage purchases
- 611121: Rent (under 61112)
- 618451: Utilities – electricity (under 61845)
