# 26.2 PCN Structure: The Seven Account Classes

### Overview of the Seven Classes

The PCN organizes all accounts into seven main classes, each representing a category of financial information. Understanding these classes is fundamental to using the PCN effectively.

### Class 1: Capital, Provisions, Financial Debts

**Purpose**: Accounts for equity, provisions, and long-term financial obligations.

**Key Groups:**
- **10**: Capital and owner's accounts (Capital)
- **11**: Share premium and similar premiums (Primes d'émission)
- **12**: Revaluation reserves (Réserves de réévaluation)
- **13**: Reserves (Réserves), including the legal reserve
- **14**: Results (Résultats): results brought forward and result for the year
- **15**: Interim dividends (Acomptes sur dividendes)
- **16**: Capital investment subsidies (Subventions d'investissement en capital)
- **18**: Provisions (Provisions)
- **19**: Financial debts (Dettes financières): bonds and bank loans

**Examples:**
- Account 101: Subscribed capital (Capital souscrit)
- Account 131: Legal reserve (Réserve légale)
- Account 188: Other provisions (Autres provisions)
- Account 1942: Bank loans due after more than one year

**Balance Sheet Impact**: Appears on the equity and liabilities side of the balance sheet.

### Class 2: Formation Expenses and Fixed Assets

**Purpose**: Accounts for formation expenses and long-term assets.

**Key Groups:**
- **20**: Formation expenses (Frais d'établissement)
- **21**: Intangible fixed assets (development costs, software, patents, goodwill)
- **22**: Tangible fixed assets (land, buildings, machinery, vehicles, furniture, IT)
- **23**: Financial fixed assets (shares in affiliated undertakings, participating interests, long-term loans)

**Examples:**
- Account 2211: Land
- Account 2213: Buildings
- Account 222: Plant and machinery
- Account 22139: Accumulated depreciation of buildings (sub-account ending in 9 under the asset)

There is no separate "accumulated depreciation" class or group in the PCN: value adjustments are kept in sub-accounts of the asset they relate to, so the asset is shown net.

**Balance Sheet Impact**: Appears on the asset side, net of value adjustments (depreciation).

### Class 3: Inventory Accounts

**Purpose**: Accounts for inventory and work in progress.

**Key Groups:**
- **30**: Raw materials and consumables
- **31**: Work in progress and contracts in progress
- **32**: Products (finished and intermediate)
- **36**: Goods for resale (Marchandises)
- **37**: Payments on account for inventories

**Examples:**
- Account 301: Raw materials (for a manufacturer)
- Account 321: Finished goods inventory
- Account 361: Merchandise (goods for resale)

Write-downs of stocks are charged to account 634 (value adjustments on inventories); the PCN has no "39" group.

**Balance Sheet Impact**: Current assets on the balance sheet.

### Class 4: Third-Party Accounts

**Purpose**: Accounts for receivables and debts with third parties (customers, suppliers, tax and social security authorities, staff).

**Key Groups:**
- **40**: Trade receivables (Créances résultant de ventes et prestations de services)
- **41**: Receivables from affiliated undertakings and undertakings with a participating interest
- **42**: Other receivables (including input VAT 421611)
- **43**: Payments received on account (customer deposits)
- **44**: Trade payables and notes payable (Dettes sur achats et prestations de services)
- **45**: Debts to affiliated undertakings and undertakings with a participating interest
- **46**: Tax and social security debts (including output VAT 461411 and CCSS 4621)
- **47**: Other debts (including net salaries owed to staff 4714)
- **48**: Accruals and deferrals (Comptes de régularisation)

**Examples:**
- Account 4011: Customers (Clients)
- Account 44111: Suppliers (Fournisseurs)
- Account 461411: Output VAT (TVA en aval)
- Account 421611: Input VAT recoverable (TVA en amont)
- Account 4621: Social security contributions payable to the CCSS

**Balance Sheet Impact**: Current assets (receivables, groups 40–42) and current liabilities (payables, groups 43–47).

### Class 5: Financial Accounts

**Purpose**: Accounts for cash, bank accounts, and short-term securities.

**Key Groups:**
- **50**: Transferable securities (Valeurs mobilières)
- **51**: Cash at bank and in hand (513 banks, 516 cash, 517 internal transfers)

**Examples:**
- Account 5131: Bank account (Banques – avoirs disponibles)
- Account 516: Cash on hand (Caisse)
- Account 517: Internal transfers (Virements internes)

**Balance Sheet Impact**: Current assets, representing liquidity.

### Class 6: Expense Accounts

**Purpose**: Accounts for all business expenses.

**Key Groups:**
- **60**: Consumption of goods and of raw materials and consumables
- **61**: Other external charges (Autres charges externes)
- **62**: Staff costs (Frais de personnel)
- **63**: Value adjustments on assets (depreciation, amortisation, write-downs)
- **64**: Other operating charges
- **65**: Financial charges
- **67**: Taxes on profit (Impôts sur le résultat)
- **68**: Other taxes not shown above (e.g., net wealth tax)

**Examples:**
- Account 6061: Purchases of goods for resale
- Account 6111: Rent of premises
- Account 6211: Gross salaries
- Account 6333: Depreciation of other fixtures, tools and equipment
- Account 6552: Bank interest

There is no group 66 ("exceptional charges"): since the 2015 reform Luxembourg GAAP has no extraordinary items.

**Income Statement Impact**: Reduces profit (appears on the expense side).

### Class 7: Revenue Accounts

**Purpose**: Accounts for all business revenues.

**Key Groups:**
- **70**: Net turnover (Montant net du chiffre d'affaires)
- **71**: Change in stocks of finished goods and work in progress
- **72**: Own work capitalised (Production immobilisée)
- **73**: Reversals of value adjustments
- **74**: Other operating income
- **75**: Financial income
- **77/78**: Adjustments of taxes on profit / of other taxes

**Examples:**
- Account 7061: Sales of goods for resale
- Account 703: Services rendered
- Account 7552: Bank interest income
- Account 744: Gains on disposal of fixed assets

There is no group 76 ("exceptional income"): disposal gains are operating income (74).

**Income Statement Impact**: Increases profit (appears on the revenue side).

### Class Structure Summary

| Class | Name | Purpose | Balance Sheet/Income Statement |
|-------|------|---------|-------------------------------|
| **1** | Capital, Provisions, Financial Debts | Equity, provisions and financial debts | Balance Sheet (Liability/Equity) |
| **2** | Formation Expenses and Fixed Assets | Long-term assets | Balance Sheet (Asset) |
| **3** | Inventory | Current inventory | Balance Sheet (Asset) |
| **4** | Third-Party Accounts | Customers, suppliers, VAT, social charges | Balance Sheet (Asset/Liability) |
| **5** | Financial Accounts | Cash and bank accounts | Balance Sheet (Asset) |
| **6** | Expense Accounts | All expenses | Income Statement (Expense) |
| **7** | Revenue Accounts | All revenues | Income Statement (Revenue) |

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

All seven classes must be used in your chart of accounts, even if some accounts remain at zero. For example, a service business may not use Class 3 (Inventory), but the class structure must be maintained. This ensures consistency and compliance with PCN requirements.

### Think It Through

Sophie's bakery purchases flour (raw material), bakes bread (production), and sells to customers. How would these transactions flow through the PCN classes? Identify which classes and specific accounts would be involved.

### Concepts in Practice

**Restaurant Example: Le Petit Bistro**

Marie's restaurant uses multiple PCN classes:

- **Class 2**: Kitchen equipment (Account 222), furniture (Account 2234)
- **Class 3**: Food inventory (Account 301), beverages (Account 361)
- **Class 4**: Customer accounts (Account 4011), supplier accounts (Account 44111), VAT (Account 461411)
- **Class 5**: Bank account (Account 5131), cash register (Account 516)
- **Class 6**: Food purchases (Account 601), rent (Account 6111), salaries (Account 6211)
- **Class 7**: Food sales (Account 703), beverage sales (Account 7061)

This structure allows Marie to track all aspects of her business systematically.
