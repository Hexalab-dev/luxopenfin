# PCN Account Reference Table

This reference table provides a comprehensive listing of the most commonly used PCN (Plan Comptable Normalisé) accounts for Luxembourg SMEs. This is based on the official PCN 2020 standard.

:::info Source
This reference is based on the Luxembourg Standard Chart of Accounts (PCN 2020) as established by the Règlement Grand-Ducal du 12 septembre 2019. For the complete official PCN, consult the Luxembourg government publications or your accounting software documentation.
:::

## Account Type Legend

- **Equity / Liability / Provision**: Equity and liabilities side of the balance sheet
- **Asset**: Asset side of the balance sheet (contra = deducted from the related asset)
- **Charge**: Expense account (appears on the profit and loss account)
- **Produit**: Revenue account (appears on the profit and loss account)

## Notes

1. **Sub-accounts**: Most accounts can have sub-accounts for detailed tracking, opened below the official account (e.g., 40111, 40112 for different customers under 4011)

2. **Custom Accounts**: Businesses can create custom sub-accounts within the PCN structure, but main account numbers must follow PCN standards

3. **Compliance**: All accounts must be classified according to PCN 2020 standards for Luxembourg compliance

4. **Complete Reference**: This table shows the most commonly used accounts. For the complete official PCN, refer to the Règlement Grand-Ducal du 12 septembre 2019 or your accounting software documentation.
