# References and Further Reading

## Official Luxembourg Sources

- **Commission des Normes Comptables (CNC)**: https://www.cnc.public.lu – Official PCN standards and updates
- **Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS)**: https://www.lbr.lu – RCS filing requirements and PCN compliance
- **Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD)**: https://www.impotsdirects.public.lu – Tax authority PCN requirements
- **Luxembourg Commercial Code (Code de Commerce)**: Legal foundation for PCN requirements

## PCN Documentation

- **PCN 2020 Official Guide**: Complete PCN account structure and classifications
- **PCN Implementation Manual**: Step-by-step guide for PCN implementation
- **PCN Chart of Accounts Template**: Standard PCN account listing

## Accounting Software Resources

- **Sage BOB Luxembourg**: https://www.sage.com – PCN-compliant accounting software
- **Odoo Luxembourg Localization**: https://www.odoo.com – Open-source PCN-compliant solution
- **Accounting Software Comparison**: Features and PCN compliance comparison

## Professional Organizations

- **Ordre des Experts-Comptables (OEC)**: https://www.oec.lu – Professional accounting body, PCN training
- **House of Entrepreneurship**: https://www.cc.lu/en/ho – Business support, PCN guidance for SMEs

## Academic and Reference Materials

- Horngren, C.T., et al. _Financial Accounting_ – Accounting principles applicable to PCN
- OpenStax _Principles of Accounting_ – Foundation accounting concepts
- Luxembourg accounting textbooks and guides

## Online Resources

- **eCDF Platform**: https://www.ecdf.lu – Electronic filing system integrated with PCN
- **Luxembourg Business Portal**: https://www.guichet.public.lu – Business registration and compliance information
- **FAIA Documentation**: Audit file format specifications

## Training and Education

- **OEC Training Programs**: PCN implementation and compliance training
- **House of Entrepreneurship Workshops**: SME accounting and PCN workshops
- **Online PCN Courses**: Web-based PCN training programs
