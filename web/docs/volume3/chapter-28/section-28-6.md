# 28.6 Luxembourg Tax Incentives for SMEs

### Understanding Tax Incentives

Luxembourg offers various **tax incentives** designed to support SMEs, encourage investment, promote innovation, and stimulate economic growth. Understanding and utilizing these incentives can significantly reduce your effective tax rate.

### Investment Tax Incentives

#### 1. Investment Allowance

**Qualifying Investments:**
- New equipment and machinery
- IT infrastructure and software
- Energy-efficient equipment
- Certain building improvements

**Allowance Rate:**
- Up to **8% investment allowance**
- Applied to qualifying investment cost
- Reduces taxable profit

**Example:**
- Equipment investment: €100,000
- Investment allowance (8%): €8,000
- Taxable profit reduction: €8,000
- **Tax savings**: €8,000 × 23.87% = €1,910

#### 2. Accelerated Depreciation

**Accelerated Depreciation Options:**
- Can accelerate depreciation in first year
- Up to 50% in first year for qualifying assets
- Reduces taxable profit in early years

**Benefits:**
- Improves cash flow in early years
- Reduces tax burden when business is growing
- Encourages investment

### Research and Development (R&D) Incentives

#### R&D Tax Credits

**Qualifying R&D Activities:**
- Research and development projects
- Innovation activities
- Technology development
- Product development

**Tax Credit Rate:**
- Up to **20% tax credit** on R&D expenses
- Reduces tax liability directly
- Can be carried forward if not fully used

**Example:**
- R&D expenses: €50,000
- Tax credit (20%): €10,000
- **Direct tax reduction**: €10,000

#### R&D Expense Deductions

**Fully Deductible:**
- R&D salaries and wages
- R&D materials and supplies
- R&D equipment (with depreciation)
- External R&D services

**Double Benefit:**
- Full deduction reduces taxable profit
- Additional tax credit reduces tax liability

### Innovation Incentives

#### IP Box Regime

**Intellectual Property Income:**
- Qualifying IP income taxed at reduced rate
- **5% tax rate** on qualifying IP income
- Significant reduction from standard rate

**Qualifying IP:**
- Patents
- Software copyrights
- Certain trademarks
- Other qualifying IP assets

**Conditions:**
- Must meet substance requirements
- Must be developed or acquired under specific conditions
- Ongoing compliance required

### Employment Incentives

#### Job Creation Incentives

**New Job Creation:**
- Tax incentives for creating new jobs
- Reduces tax burden when hiring
- Encourages employment growth

**Qualifying Conditions:**
- New permanent positions created
- Specific job types may qualify
- Must meet certain criteria

### Regional Development Incentives

#### Location-Based Incentives

**Certain Regions:**
- Special incentives for businesses in specific regions
- Encourages regional development
- May include tax reductions or credits

### SME-Specific Incentives

#### Small Business Deductions

**SME Benefits:**
- Reduced rates for small businesses
- Special deductions for SMEs
- Simplified compliance for small businesses

#### Start-Up Incentives

**New Business Benefits:**
- Special deductions for new businesses
- Reduced rates in early years
- Encourages entrepreneurship

### How to Access Incentives

#### Step 1: Identify Qualifying Activities

- Review your business activities
- Identify qualifying investments
- Identify R&D activities
- Identify other qualifying activities

#### Step 2: Maintain Documentation

- Keep detailed records of qualifying expenses
- Document R&D activities
- Maintain investment documentation
- Keep all supporting documents

#### Step 3: Claim in Tax Return

- Include in annual tax return
- Provide required documentation
- Calculate credits and allowances
- Submit with tax return

#### Step 4: Professional Support

- Consult tax advisor
- Ensure proper qualification
- Maximize available incentives
- Maintain compliance

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

**Important Requirements:**
- **Qualification**: Ensure activities qualify for incentives
- **Documentation**: Maintain proper documentation
- **Compliance**: Meet all compliance requirements
- **Substance**: Meet substance requirements (for IP Box)
- **Professional advice**: Consult tax advisor for complex incentives

**Common Issues:**
- **Missing opportunities**: Not identifying qualifying activities
- **Insufficient documentation**: Not maintaining proper records
- **Compliance failures**: Not meeting compliance requirements
- **Incorrect claims**: Claiming incentives incorrectly

### Think It Through

*TechLux Solutions* spends €100,000 on R&D activities and invests €200,000 in new equipment. What tax incentives can they access? How much can they save?

### Concepts in Practice

**Maximizing Tax Incentives**

*TechLux Solutions* tax incentive planning:

**R&D Activities:**
- R&D expenses: €100,000
- Tax credit (20%): €20,000
- **Direct tax reduction**: €20,000

**Equipment Investment:**
- Equipment investment: €200,000
- Investment allowance (8%): €16,000
- Taxable profit reduction: €16,000
- **Tax savings**: €16,000 × 23.87% = €3,819

**Total Tax Benefits:**
- R&D tax credit: €20,000
- Investment allowance savings: €3,819
- **Total tax reduction**: €23,819

**Effective Tax Rate Reduction:**
- Without incentives: 23.87% (Luxembourg City, from 2025)
- With incentives: Significantly reduced
