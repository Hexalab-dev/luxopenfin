# Chapter 28 – Exercises & Cases

## Multiple Choice Questions

1. Luxembourg's standard corporate income tax rate (taxable income above €200,000, tax years 2025–2026) is:
   a) 15%
   b) 16%
   c) 17%
   d) 20%
   **Answer:** b) Luxembourg's standard corporate income tax rate is 16% from tax year 2025 (17% for 2024 and earlier).

2. Municipal business tax in Luxembourg City is:
   a) 6.0%
   b) 6.75%
   c) 7.0%
   d) 7.5%
   **Answer:** b) Municipal business tax in Luxembourg City is 6.75%.

3. The effective combined tax rate in Luxembourg City (from 2025) is approximately:
   a) 20%
   b) 23.87%
   c) 24.94%
   d) 30%
   **Answer:** b) The effective combined tax rate in Luxembourg City is 23.87% from 2025 (24.94% for 2024 and earlier).

4. The corporate tax return for a calendar year must be filed by:
   a) 31 May of the following year
   b) 31 July of the following year
   c) 30 September of the following year
   d) 31 December of the following year
   **Answer:** d) The corporate tax return (modèle 500) is due 31 December of the following year. (31 July is the latest date for filing the annual accounts with the RCS.)

5. Since tax year 2024, the investment tax credit (art. 152bis LIR) for investments in digital transformation or the ecological and energy transition is:
   a) 5%
   b) 8%
   c) 12%
   d) 15%
   **Answer:** c) 12% of the qualifying investment. It is a tax credit: it reduces the tax due, not the taxable income.

6. Luxembourg's main support for business R&D is:
   a) A 20% R&D tax credit
   b) R&D grants (RDI aid from the Ministry of the Economy, via Luxinnovation), with R&D costs deductible as normal business expenses
   c) A 150% super-deduction of R&D costs
   d) No support at all
   **Answer:** b) Luxembourg has no general R&D tax credit; R&D is supported mainly through grants.

7. Deferred tax assets arise when:
   a) Accounting expenses exceed tax deductions
   b) Tax deductions exceed accounting expenses
   c) Revenue is recognized for accounting before tax
   d) Tax rate increases
   **Answer:** a) Deferred tax assets arise when accounting expenses exceed tax deductions.

8. Transfer pricing rules require:
   a) Related parties to charge market rates
   b) Arm's length pricing
   c) Cost-plus pricing only
   d) No pricing restrictions
   **Answer:** b) Transfer pricing rules require arm's length pricing.

9. Tax losses can be carried forward for:
   a) 5 years
   b) 10 years
   c) 17 years
   d) 20 years
   **Answer:** c) Tax losses arising from 2017 onwards can be carried forward for 17 years (older losses without limit).

10. The IP box (art. 50ter LIR) exempts 80% of qualifying net IP income. At the combined Luxembourg City rate of 23.87% (from 2025), the effective rate on that income is about:
    a) 3%
    b) 4.8%
    c) 8%
    d) 10%
    **Answer:** b) About 4.77% (20% × 23.87%).
