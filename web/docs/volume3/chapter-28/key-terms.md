# Key Terms

- **Corporate Income Tax (CIT) / Impôt sur le Revenu des Collectivités (IRC)**: Tax on corporate taxable profits in Luxembourg (tax years 2025–2026: 14% up to €175,000, 16% above €200,000, with a transition band in between; 17% top rate for 2024 and earlier)
- **Municipal Business Tax (ICC) / Impôt Commercial Communal**: Local tax on business profits, varies by municipality (6.75% in Luxembourg City)
- **Solidarity Surcharge (Employment Fund Contribution)**: 7% of corporate income tax (1.12% effective rate at the 16% CIT rate)
- **Combined Tax Rate**: Total effective tax rate including CIT, solidarity surcharge and municipal business tax (23.87% in Luxembourg City from 2025; 24.94% for 2024 and earlier)
- **Taxable Profit**: Profit subject to tax (accounting profit ± tax adjustments)
- **Tax Adjustments**: Differences between accounting profit and taxable profit
- **Tax Deductions**: Expenses that reduce taxable profit
- **Investment Allowance**: Tax allowance of up to 8% on qualifying investments
- **Accelerated Depreciation**: Depreciation method allowing higher depreciation in early years
- **R&D Tax Credit**: Tax credit of up to 20% on R&D expenses
- **IP Box Regime**: Reduced tax rate (5%) on qualifying intellectual property income
- **Tax Loss Carry-Forward**: Ability to use tax losses to reduce future taxable profit (17 years for losses from 2017 onwards; older losses unlimited)
- **Deferred Tax Asset**: Future tax benefit from temporary differences (accounting expenses exceed tax deductions)
- **Deferred Tax Liability**: Future tax obligation from temporary differences (tax deductions exceed accounting expenses)
- **Temporary Differences**: Differences between accounting and tax treatment that reverse over time
- **Transfer Pricing**: Pricing of transactions between related parties
- **Arm's Length Principle**: Requirement that related-party transactions be priced as if between independent parties
- **Transfer Pricing Documentation**: Documentation required for related-party transactions (master file, local file)
- **Tax Filing Deadline**: Corporate tax return (modèle 500) due 31 December of the year following the tax year (annual accounts: filed with the RCS at the latest 7 months after year-end)
- **eCDF**: Electronic platform used for annual accounts and VAT returns in Luxembourg (corporate tax returns are filed via MyGuichet.lu)
