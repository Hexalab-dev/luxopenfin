# Chapter 28 – Solutions

## Multiple Choice Solutions

1. **b)** Luxembourg's standard corporate income tax rate is 16% from tax year 2025 (17% for 2024 and earlier).

2. **b)** Municipal business tax in Luxembourg City is 6.75%.

3. **b)** The effective combined tax rate in Luxembourg City is 23.87% from 2025 (24.94% for 2024 and earlier).

4. **d)** The corporate tax return is due 31 December of the following year (annual accounts: within 7 months of year-end).

5. **c)** 12% investment tax credit (since 2024) for digital-transformation and ecological/energy-transition investments; it reduces the tax due.

6. **b)** Luxembourg has no general R&D tax credit; R&D is supported mainly by grants (RDI aid), and R&D costs are deductible as normal expenses.

7. **a)** Deferred tax assets arise when accounting expenses exceed tax deductions.

8. **b)** Transfer pricing rules require arm's length pricing.

9. **c)** Tax losses from 2017 onwards can be carried forward for 17 years (older losses without limit).

10. **b)** About 4.77%: 80% of qualifying net IP income is exempt, so 20% × 23.87%.
