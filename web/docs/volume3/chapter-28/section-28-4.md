# 28.4 Tax Filing Requirements and Deadlines

### Understanding Tax Filing Obligations

All companies in Luxembourg must file corporate tax returns and pay taxes on time. Understanding filing requirements and deadlines is essential for compliance and avoiding penalties.

### Filing Requirements

#### Who Must File

**All Companies Must File:**
- All registered companies (SA, SARL, SCS, SNC, etc.)
- Regardless of profit or loss
- Even if no tax is due
- Must file annual tax return

#### What Must Be Filed

**Required Documents:**
1. **Corporate Tax Return**: Complete tax return form
2. **Financial Statements**: Annual accounts (balance sheet, income statement)
3. **Supporting Schedules**: Detailed schedules and calculations
4. **Supporting Documentation**: Invoices, contracts, etc. (if requested)

### Filing Deadlines

#### Standard Deadline

**Corporate Tax Return (modèle 500, covering IRC, ICC and net wealth tax)**: due **31 December of the year following** the tax year

**Example:**
- Fiscal year-end: December 31, 2024
- Tax return deadline: December 31, 2025

**Do not confuse** this with the **annual accounts** deadline: accounts are approved within 6 months of year-end and filed with the RCS within one month of approval, i.e. at the latest **7 months** after year-end (July 31, 2025 for a calendar year). The approved accounts are needed to prepare the tax return.

#### Payment Deadline

**Advance payments**: Quarterly advances fixed by the tax administration (ACD) are paid during the year.

**Balance**: Any remaining tax is due after the assessment is issued (generally by the end of the month following receipt of the assessment).

### Extension Requests

#### Requesting Extensions

**Extension Available:**
- The tax administration may grant extra time on a justified request
- Request it before the original deadline
- Confirm the practice and any conditions with your tax adviser

### Tax Return Contents

#### Required Information

**Tax Return Must Include:**
1. **Company Information**: Name, address, RCS number, tax ID
2. **Fiscal Period**: Start and end dates
3. **Revenue**: Total revenue and breakdown
4. **Expenses**: Total expenses and major categories
5. **Tax Adjustments**: All adjustments to accounting profit
6. **Taxable Profit**: Calculated taxable profit
7. **Tax Calculation**: Corporate income tax, municipal tax, employment fund
8. **Tax Credits**: Any applicable tax credits
9. **Tax Due**: Final tax liability

#### Supporting Schedules

**Common Schedules:**
- Depreciation schedule
- Interest expense schedule
- Related-party transactions
- Tax loss carry-forwards
- Investment allowances

### Electronic Filing

#### MyGuichet.lu and eCDF

**Electronic Filing:**
- Corporate tax returns (modèle 500) are filed electronically via MyGuichet.lu; electronic filing is mandatory
- eCDF is used for the annual accounts (balance sheet and profit and loss account, then filed with the RCS via LBR) and for VAT returns
- Paper returns generally not accepted

#### Filing Process

**Steps:**
1. Finalise and approve the annual accounts
2. Log in to MyGuichet.lu
3. Complete the corporate tax return (modèle 500)
4. Attach the required schedules and documents
5. Review and validate
6. Submit electronically
7. Pay the balance once the assessment is received

### Payment Methods

#### Payment Options

**Available Methods:**
1. **Bank Transfer**: To the tax administration's (ACD) account, quoting the reference on the advance-payment notice or assessment
2. **Online Banking**: Standing orders for recurring quarterly advances

#### Payment Confirmation

**After Payment:**
- Keep the bank confirmation for your records
- Reconcile payments against the ACD account statement

### Late Filing and Payment Penalties

#### Late Filing Penalties

**Penalties:**
- **€250 - €2,500**: Fixed penalty for late filing
- **Additional penalties**: For very late filing
- **Interest**: On unpaid tax from due date

#### Late Payment Penalties

**Penalties:**
- **Interest**: Charged on overdue amounts
- **Additional penalty**: For persistent non-payment
- **Collection action**: For serious non-payment

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

**Critical Requirements:**
- **File on time**: Late filing results in penalties
- **Pay on time**: Late payment results in interest and penalties
- **Accurate returns**: Errors can trigger audits
- **Keep records**: Maintain all supporting documentation
- **Electronic filing**: Tax return via MyGuichet.lu; annual accounts via eCDF/LBR

**Best Practices:**
- **Start early**: Begin preparation well before deadline
- **Organize documents**: Keep all tax documents organized
- **Review carefully**: Review return before submission
- **Professional help**: Use tax advisor for complex situations
- **Calendar reminders**: Set reminders for deadlines

### Think It Through

*TechLux Solutions* has a fiscal year ending December 31. Their year-end closing is delayed, and the annual accounts may not be approved and filed by July 31. How does this affect the tax return due December 31? What are their options?

### Concepts in Practice

**Tax Filing Process**

*TechLux Solutions* tax filing:

**Fiscal Year**: January 1 - December 31, 2024
**Annual Accounts Filing Deadline (RCS)**: July 31, 2025
**Tax Return Deadline**: December 31, 2025

**Preparation Timeline:**
- January-February: Gather documents
- March-April: Prepare financial statements
- By June 30: Shareholders approve the accounts
- By July 31: File the accounts with the RCS (eCDF/LBR)
- Autumn: Calculate tax adjustments and complete the tax return
- By December 31: Review and file the tax return

**Filing Process:**
1. Complete tax return form (modèle 500)
2. Prepare supporting schedules
3. Review all calculations
4. Log in to MyGuichet.lu
5. Enter return information
6. Attach supporting documents
7. Review and validate
8. Submit electronically
9. Pay the balance after the assessment
10. Keep confirmations

**Result**: Return filed on time, tax paid, no penalties.
