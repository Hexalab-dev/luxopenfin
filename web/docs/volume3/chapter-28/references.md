# References and Further Reading

## Official Luxembourg Sources

- **Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD)**: https://www.impotsdirects.public.lu – Official tax authority, corporate tax guidance
- **Luxembourg Tax Code**: Complete tax legislation including corporate tax
- **Municipal Tax Rates**: Official municipal business tax rates by municipality
- **Tax Incentive Guides**: Official guides on tax incentives and allowances

## Tax Guidance and Resources

- **ACD Corporate Tax Guides**: Official guides on corporate tax calculation and filing
- **Tax Incentive Documentation**: Guides on accessing tax incentives
- **Transfer Pricing Guidelines**: Official transfer pricing guidelines
- **eCDF Platform**: https://www.ecdf.lu – Electronic tax return filing system

## Professional Organizations

- **Ordre des Experts-Comptables (OEC)**: https://www.oec.lu – Professional accounting body, tax training and guidance
- **House of Entrepreneurship**: https://www.cc.lu/en/ho – Business support, tax guidance for SMEs
- **Chamber of Commerce**: https://www.cc.lu – Business resources, tax information

## International Tax Resources

- **OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines**: International transfer pricing standards
- **EU Tax Directives**: EU tax rules and directives
- **Double Taxation Treaties**: Luxembourg's tax treaty network

## Academic and Reference Materials

- **Luxembourg Tax Law**: Complete tax legislation
- **Corporate Tax Planning Guides**: Practical guides for tax planning
- **Transfer Pricing Guides**: Guides for transfer pricing compliance
- **Tax Accounting Standards**: IFRS and Luxembourg GAAP tax accounting

## Online Resources

- **ACD Online Services**: https://www.impotsdirects.public.lu – Online tax services and information
- **Luxembourg Business Portal**: https://www.guichet.public.lu – Business registration and tax information
- **Tax Calculators**: Online tools for tax calculations

## Training and Education

- **OEC Tax Training**: Professional tax training programs
- **House of Entrepreneurship Workshops**: SME tax workshops
- **Online Tax Courses**: Web-based tax training
- **ACD Seminars**: Tax authority tax seminars
