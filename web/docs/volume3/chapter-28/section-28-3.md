# 28.3 Municipal Business Tax (Impôt Commercial)

### Understanding Municipal Business Tax

**Municipal Business Tax (ICC)**—**Impôt Commercial Communal**—is a local tax levied by municipalities on business profits. It's the second major component of Luxembourg's corporate tax system, alongside corporate income tax.

### Tax Rate Structure

#### Base Rate and Multiplier

Municipal business tax uses a **base rate** multiplied by a **municipal coefficient**:

**Base Rate**: 3% (set by national law)

**Municipal Coefficient**: Varies by municipality (typically 200% - 250%)

**Effective Rate Calculation:**
- Effective Rate = Base Rate × Municipal Coefficient
- Example (Luxembourg City): 3% × 225% = 6.75%
- Example (Other municipality): 3% × 250% = 7.5%

### Municipal Rate Variations

#### Luxembourg City

**Rate**: 6.75% (3% × 225%)

**Applies to**: Businesses located in Luxembourg City

#### Other Municipalities

**Rates**: Typically 6.0% - 7.5%

**Variation**: Each municipality sets its own coefficient

**Important**: Check your specific municipality's rate

### Tax Base

#### Same as Corporate Income Tax

Municipal business tax is calculated on the **same taxable profit** as corporate income tax:

**Tax Base = Taxable Profit (same as for CIT)**

**Calculation:**
- Taxable profit: €100,000
- Municipal business tax (6.75%): €6,750

### Combined Tax Impact

#### Total Corporate Tax Burden

**Components:**
1. Corporate Income Tax: 16% (standard rate from 2025; 17% for 2024 and earlier)
2. Municipal Business Tax: 6.75% (varies)
3. Solidarity surcharge (Employment Fund): 1.12% (7% of CIT)

**Combined Effective Rate:**
- Luxembourg City: 23.87% from 2025 (24.94% for 2024 and earlier)
- Other municipalities: Varies with the municipal multiplier

### Municipal Tax Calculation

#### Step 1: Determine Taxable Profit

- Same taxable profit as for corporate income tax
- After all deductions and adjustments

#### Step 2: Apply Municipal Rate

- Multiply taxable profit by municipal rate
- Rate depends on municipality location

#### Step 3: Calculate Municipal Tax

- Municipal Tax = Taxable Profit × Municipal Rate

**Example:**
- Taxable profit: €200,000
- Municipal rate (Luxembourg City): 6.75%
- Municipal business tax: €13,500

### Payment and Filing

#### Combined Filing

- Municipal business tax filed together with corporate income tax
- Single tax return covers both taxes
- Same filing deadline applies

#### Payment

- Paid together with corporate income tax
- Single payment covers both taxes
- Same payment deadline applies

### Municipal Tax Planning

#### Location Considerations

**Municipality Selection:**
- Different municipalities have different rates
- Lower rates can reduce tax burden
- Consider other factors (rent, accessibility, etc.)

**Rate Comparison:**
- Luxembourg City: 6.75%
- Other municipalities: 6.0% - 7.5%
- Difference can be significant for large profits

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

**Important Requirements:**
- **Correct municipality**: Use correct rate for your location
- **Combined filing**: File together with corporate income tax
- **Accurate calculation**: Ensure accurate taxable profit calculation
- **Timely payment**: Pay by deadline to avoid penalties
- **Rate verification**: Verify current municipal rate annually

**Common Issues:**
- **Wrong municipality rate**: Using incorrect rate for location
- **Calculation errors**: Errors in taxable profit calculation
- **Timing issues**: Incorrect recognition of income/expenses
- **Missing adjustments**: Not applying all tax adjustments
