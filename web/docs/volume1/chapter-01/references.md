# References and Further Reading

## Official Luxembourg Sources

- **Guichet.lu**: https://guichet.public.lu  
  Comprehensive business information portal detailing accounting requirements, PCN obligations, and administrative procedures.

- **Commission des Normes Comptables (CNC)**: https://cnc.lu  
  Official PCN documentation, accounting standards, and interpretative guidance.

- **Registre de Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS)**: https://www.rcsl.lu  
  Company registration, annual account filing, and public company information.

- **Ordre des Experts-Comptables (OEC)**: https://www.oec.lu  
  Professional organization for Luxembourg accountants; certification standards and career resources.

- **Administration des Contributions Directes (ACD)**: https://impotsdirects.public.lu  
  Tax authority information covering VAT, corporate income tax, and filing obligations.

## Educational Resources

- **House of Training**: https://www.houseoftraining.lu  
  Professional training courses, including accounting and finance programs tailored to Luxembourg practice.

- **University of Luxembourg**: https://www.uni.lu  
  Degree programs, research, and publications in accounting and finance.

## Additional Reading

- *Précis de droit comptable* by Denis Colin (Legitech) – comprehensive Luxembourg accounting reference (French).
- OpenStax *Principles of Financial Accounting* – freely available foundational text for universal accounting principles.

## Key Regulations

- Commercial Code (Code de Commerce)
- Plan Comptable Normalisé 2020 (PCN 2020)
- VAT Law (Loi sur la TVA)
- Corporate Income Tax Law
