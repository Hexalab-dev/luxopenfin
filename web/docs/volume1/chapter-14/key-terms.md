# Key Terms

- **Dividends**: Distributions to shareholders
- **Earnings per Share (EPS)**: Net income per share
- **Retained Earnings**: Accumulated profits not distributed
- **Share Capital**: Amount invested by owners
- **Stock Dividend**: Distribution of additional shares
- **Stock Split**: Increase shares, decrease par value proportionally
- **Treasury Stock**: Company's own shares repurchased
