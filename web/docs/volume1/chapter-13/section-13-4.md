# 13.4 Appendix: Special Topics Related to Long-Term Liabilities

### Special Topics

**Lease Obligations:**
- Operating leases vs. finance leases
- Finance leases recorded as liabilities
- Must comply with lease accounting standards

**Convertible Debt:**
- Debt that can be converted to equity
- Special accounting considerations
- Less common for SMEs

**Debt Restructuring:**
- Modifying debt terms
- Special accounting treatment
- May involve gain or loss

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

Special topics in Luxembourg:
- Must follow accounting standards
- Must comply with regulations
- Must maintain proper documentation
- Must use appropriate PCN accounts
