# 13.6 Luxembourg Loan Accounting and Interest Expense

### Loan Accounting

**Loan Receipt:**
- Record loan as liability
- Record cash received
- Set up amortization schedule

**Interest Accrual:**
- Accrue interest over time
- Use effective-interest method
- Record interest expense

**Loan Payment:**
- Record principal and interest
- Reduce loan balance
- Record interest expense

### Luxembourg Considerations

**Tax Treatment:**
- Interest expense is tax-deductible
- Must be properly documented
- Affects taxable income

**Documentation:**
- Loan agreements
- Amortization schedules
- Payment records
- Interest calculations

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

Loan accounting in Luxembourg:
- Must use proper PCN accounts
- Must accrue interest properly
- Must maintain documentation
- Interest expense affects taxes
- Must comply with regulations
