# Key Terms

- **Bank Reconciliation**: Process of comparing bank statement to cash records
- **Deposits in Transit**: Deposits made but not yet on bank statement
- **Fraud**: Intentional deception for personal gain
- **Fraud Triangle**: Three factors (pressure, opportunity, rationalization) that lead to fraud
- **Internal Controls**: Policies and procedures to safeguard assets and ensure accuracy
- **Misappropriation of Assets**: Theft of company assets
- **Outstanding Checks**: Checks written but not yet cashed
- **Petty Cash**: Small amount of cash for incidental expenses
- **SEPA**: Single Euro Payments Area - standardized euro payment system
- **Tone at the Top**: Management's attitude toward controls and ethics
