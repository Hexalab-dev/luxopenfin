# 15.6 Luxembourg Partnership Forms (SNC, SCS)

### SNC (Société en Nom Collectif)

**Characteristics:**
- General partnership
- All partners have unlimited liability
- Partners manage business
- Less common

### SCS (Société en Commandite Simple)

**Characteristics:**
- Limited partnership
- General partners: unlimited liability, manage business
- Limited partners: limited liability, no management
- More common than SNC

### Accounting Differences

**Both Use:**
- Partner capital accounts
- Profit/loss allocation
- Similar accounting principles

**Differences:**
- Liability structure
- Management rights
- Some legal requirements

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

Partnership forms in Luxembourg:
- SNC: General partnership
- SCS: Limited partnership
- Must comply with partnership law
- Must use proper PCN accounts
- Must file with RCS
