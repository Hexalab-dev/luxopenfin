# Key Terms

- **Capital Account**: Partner's equity in partnership
- **Partnership**: Business owned by two or more partners
- **Partnership Agreement**: Contract defining partnership terms
- **Profit Allocation**: Distribution of profits to partners
- **SNC**: Société en Nom Collectif (general partnership)
- **SCS**: Société en Commandite Simple (limited partnership)
