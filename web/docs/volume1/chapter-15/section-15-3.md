# 15.3 Compute and Allocate Partners' Share of Income and Loss

### Allocation Methods

**1. Equal Shares:**
- Each partner gets equal share
- Simple and common

**2. Capital Ratio:**
- Based on capital contributions
- Proportional to investment

**3. Service Ratio:**
- Based on effort/contribution
- May vary by partner

**4. Combination:**
- Mix of factors
- Defined in agreement

### Example: Capital Ratio

**Capital Balances:**
- Partner A: €60,000
- Partner B: €40,000
- Total: €100,000

**Ratios:**
- Partner A: 60%
- Partner B: 40%

**Net Income: €50,000**
- Partner A: €50,000 × 60% = €30,000
- Partner B: €50,000 × 40% = €20,000

**Journal Entry:**
```
142    Income Summary             €50,000
1041   Partner A, Capital                      €30,000
1042   Partner B, Capital                      €20,000
To allocate net income by capital ratio
```

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

Income allocation in Luxembourg:
- Must follow agreement
- Must be documented
- Must use proper accounts
- Must comply with law
