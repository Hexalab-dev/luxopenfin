# 12.6 Luxembourg Social Charges and Payroll Liabilities

### Social Charges in Luxembourg

**Social Charges** include:
- Employee social security contributions
- Employer social security contributions
- Health insurance
- Pension contributions
- Dependency (long-term care) insurance (employee only)
- Accident insurance, health at work and Mutualité des employeurs (employer only)

**CCSS rates as of 1 January 2026** (check ccss.lu each year, as they change):

| Contribution | Employee | Employer |
|---|---|---|
| Health (benefits in kind + cash) | 2.80% + 0.25% | 2.80% + 0.25% |
| Pension | 8.50% | 8.50% |
| Dependency insurance | 1.40% | – |
| Accident insurance | – | 0.65% |
| Health at work (STM) | – | 0.14% |
| Mutualité des employeurs (class 1–4) | – | 0.23%–2.66% |
| **Total (examples: Mutualité class 1)** | **12.95%** | **12.57%** |

Dependency insurance is legally computed on gross salary minus 1/4 of the social minimum wage; the examples below simplify by applying 1.40% to the full gross.

### Employee Social Charges

**Employee Portion:**
- Deducted from gross salary
- Payable by employer
- Various rates apply

**Example:**
- Gross Salary: €5,000
- Employee Social Charges: €647.50 (12.95%)

**Journal Entry:**
```
6211   Salaries Expense           €5,000
4714   Salaries Payable                        €4,352.50
4621   Employee Social Charges Payable         €647.50
To record salary with employee social charges
```

### Employer Social Charges

**Employer Portion:**
- Additional cost to employer
- Not deducted from employee
- Various rates apply

**Example:**
- Gross Salary: €5,000
- Employer Social Charges: €628.50 (12.57%)

**Journal Entry:**
```
6211   Salaries Expense           €628.50
4621   Employer Social Charges Payable         €628.50
To record employer social charges
```

### Total Payroll Cost

**Total Cost:**
- Gross Salary: €5,000 (already includes the €647.50 employee charges withheld)
- Employer Social Charges: €628.50
- **Total Cost: €5,628.50**

### Payment of Social Charges

**Payment:**
```
4621   Employee Social Charges Payable €647.50
4621   Employer Social Charges Payable    628.50
5131   Cash                                   €1,276.00
To record payment of social charges
```

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

Social charges in Luxembourg:
- Must be calculated correctly
- Must be paid on time
- Must comply with rates
- Must maintain records
- Penalties for late payment
- Must use proper PCN accounts
