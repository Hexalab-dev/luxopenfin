# 12.4 Luxembourg Current Liabilities (PCN Class 4 - Fournisseurs)

### PCN Class 4: Tiers (Third Parties)

**Class 4** contains all third-party obligations.

**Account Ranges:**
- 44: Suppliers (Fournisseurs)
- 40: Customers (Clients)
- 42 / 47: Other Third Parties
- 4216 / 4614: VAT Accounts (TVA)
- 462: Social Charges (Charges Sociales)
- 461: Tax Accounts (Impôts)
- 47: Other Liabilities (Autres Dettes)
- 48: Deferred Revenue (Produits Différés)

### Supplier Accounts (44)

**Main Accounts:**
- **44111**: Accounts Payable - Suppliers
- **4421**: Notes Payable to Suppliers
- **4421**: Bills of Exchange Payable
- **44113**: Advances to Suppliers

### VAT Accounts (4216 / 4614)

**Main Accounts:**
- **461411**: VAT Payable
- **421611**: VAT Recoverable
- **461412**: VAT Due
- **421612**: VAT Credit

### Social Charges (462)

**Main Accounts:**
- **4714**: Salaries Payable
- **4621**: Social Charges Payable
- **4621**: Employee Social Charges Payable
- **4621**: Employer Social Charges Payable

### Tax Accounts (461)

**Main Accounts:**
- **46121**: Income Tax Payable
- **46122**: Municipal Business Tax Payable
- **461**: Other Taxes Payable

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

PCN Class 4 requirements:
- Must use proper account classifications
- Must maintain accurate records
- Must support all balances
- Must comply with PCN structure
- Must enable proper reporting
