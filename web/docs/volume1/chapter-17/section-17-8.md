# 17.8 Luxembourg SME Performance Indicators

### Key Indicators

**For Luxembourg SMEs:**
- Profitability ratios
- Liquidity ratios
- Efficiency ratios
- Growth indicators

### Typical Values

**Restaurant Industry:**
- Gross margin: 30-50%
- Net margin: 5-15%
- Current ratio: &gt;1.5

**Retail Industry:**
- Gross margin: 40-60%
- Net margin: 3-10%
- Inventory turnover: 4-6 times

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

Performance indicators in Luxembourg:
- Vary by industry
- Help evaluate performance
- Compare to benchmarks
- Support decisions
