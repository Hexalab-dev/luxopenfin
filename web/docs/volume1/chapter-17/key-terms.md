# Key Terms

- **Common-Size Statement**: Financial statement with items as percentages
- **Comparative Statement**: Financial statement showing multiple periods
- **Financial Ratio**: Relationship between two financial statement items
- **Return on Assets (ROA)**: Net income as percentage of assets
- **Return on Equity (ROE)**: Net income as percentage of equity
