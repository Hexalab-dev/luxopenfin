# 2.4 Luxembourg PCN Account Classifications (Classes 1-7)

### Understanding PCN Structure

The **Plan Comptable Normalisé (PCN)** is Luxembourg's standardized chart of accounts. All businesses must use PCN classifications when preparing financial statements. PCN 2020 organizes accounts into seven main classes.

### Class 1: Capital Accounts, Provisions, and Financial Debts (10–19)

**Purpose:** Accounts related to ownership, reserves, and long-term financing

**Key Accounts:**
- **101**: Share Capital (Capital Social)
- **111**: Share Premium (Prime d'Émission)
- **138**: Reserves (Réserves)
- **141**: Retained Earnings (Bénéfices Reportés)
- **142**: Current Year Profit/Loss (Résultat de l'Exercice)
- **18**: Provisions (Provisions)
- **19**: Financial Debts (Dettes Financières)

**Appears on:** Balance Sheet (Equity and Long-Term Liabilities sections)

**Example:** A SARL with €50,000 share capital and €20,000 retained earnings would show:
- Class 1 accounts on Balance Sheet under Equity

### Class 2: Fixed Assets and Establishment Costs (20–29)

**Purpose:** Long-term assets and startup costs

**Key Accounts:**
- **20**: Establishment Costs (Frais d'Établissement)
- **21**: Intangible Assets (Immobilisations Incorporelles)
- **22**: Tangible Assets (Immobilisations Corporelles)
  - 2211: Land (Terrains)
  - 2213: Buildings (Bâtiments)
  - 222: Machinery and Equipment (Matériel)
  - 2232: Vehicles (Véhicules)
- **23**: Financial Fixed Assets (Immobilisations Financières)
- **…9 sub-accounts**: Accumulated Depreciation (Amortissements)

**Appears on:** Balance Sheet (Noncurrent Assets section)

**Example:** Restaurant equipment (€30,000) is classified as:
- 222: Machinery and Equipment (Class 2)

### Class 3: Inventory Accounts (30–39)

**Purpose:** Goods and materials held for sale or production

**Key Accounts:**
- **30**: Raw Materials (Matières Premières)
- **31**: Work in Progress (En Cours de Fabrication)
- **32**: Finished Goods (Produits Finis)
- **361**: Merchandise (Marchandises)

**Appears on:** Balance Sheet (Current Assets section)

**Example:** A retail store's inventory of €25,000 is classified as:
- 361: Merchandise (Class 3)

### Class 4: Third-Party Accounts (40–49)

**Purpose:** Accounts with customers, suppliers, employees, and tax authorities

**Key Accounts:**
- **44**: Suppliers (Fournisseurs)
  - 44111: Trade Payables
- **40**: Customers (Clients)
  - 4011: Trade Receivables
- **462**: Social Security (Sécurité Sociale)
  - 4621: Social Security Payable
- **421 / 461**: Tax Authorities (Administrations Fiscales)
  - 461411: VAT Payable (TVA à Payer)
  - 421611: VAT Recoverable (TVA Récupérable)
  - 46121: Income Tax Payable
- **4621 / 4714**: Personnel (Personnel)
  - 4714: Salaries Payable
- **42 / 47**: Other Third Parties

**Appears on:** Balance Sheet (Current Assets and Current Liabilities sections)

**Example:** 
- €15,000 owed by customers: 4011 (Class 4 - Current Asset)
- €8,000 owed to suppliers: 44111 (Class 4 - Current Liability)
- €2,000 VAT payable: 461411 (Class 4 - Current Liability)

### Class 5: Financial Accounts (50–59)

**Purpose:** Cash and bank accounts

**Key Accounts:**
- **516**: Cash (Liquidités)
  - 516: Petty Cash (Caisse)
  - 516: Cash on Hand
- **513**: Bank Accounts (Comptes Bancaires)
  - 5131: Current Account (Compte Courant)
  - 5131: Payment Cards/Cetrel Account
- **50**: Financial Instruments (Valeurs Mobilières)
- **51**: Other Financial Accounts

**Appears on:** Balance Sheet (Current Assets section)

**Example:** 
- €25,000 in bank account: 5131 (Class 5 - Current Asset)
- €500 petty cash: 516 (Class 5 - Current Asset)

### Class 6: Expense Accounts (60–69)

**Purpose:** All business expenses

**Key Accounts:**
- **60**: Purchases (Achats)
  - 6061: Merchandise Purchases
- **61**: External Services (Services Extérieurs)
  - 6134: Professional Services
  - 6111: Rent (Loyers)
  - 614: Insurance (Assurances)
  - 6184: Utilities (Services Publics)
  - 6188: Other External Services
- **62**: Personnel Expenses (Charges de Personnel)
  - 6211: Salaries (Salaires)
  - 623: Social Charges (Employer portion)
- **64 / 67**: Taxes (Impôts et Taxes)
- **63**: Depreciation (Amortissements)
- **64**: Other Operating Expenses
- **65**: Financial Expenses (Charges Financières)
  - 6552: Interest Expense

**Appears on:** Income Statement (Expenses section)

**Example:**
- €25,000 salaries: 6211 (Class 6 - Expense)
- €6,000 rent: 6111 (Class 6 - Expense)
- €1,500 utilities: 6184 (Class 6 - Expense)

### Class 7: Revenue Accounts (70–79)

**Purpose:** All business revenues

**Key Accounts:**
- **70**: Sales (Ventes)
  - 7061: Sales of Goods
  - 703: Sales of Services
- **71–72**: Production (Production)
- **74**: Other Operating Revenue
- **75**: Financial Revenue (Produits Financiers)
  - 7552: Interest Income

**Appears on:** Income Statement (Revenue section)

**Example:**
- €50,000 service revenue: 703 (Class 7 - Revenue)

### PCN and Financial Statement Preparation

When preparing financial statements in Luxembourg:

1. **All accounts must be classified according to PCN**
2. **Account numbers should be shown** (in parentheses or separate column)
3. **Statements must follow PCN format** for presentation
4. **Account descriptions** should use PCN terminology

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

All financial statements filed with RCS must:
- Use PCN account classifications
- Follow PCN presentation format
- Include proper account numbers
- Use PCN terminology (in French, German, or English)

Non-compliance can result in rejection of filing and penalties.
