# 16.5 Use Information from the Statement of Cash Flows to Prepare Ratios to Assess Liquidity and Solvency

### Cash Flow Ratios

**Operating Cash Flow Ratio:**
**Operating Cash Flow Ratio = Cash from Operations ÷ Current Liabilities**

**Free Cash Flow:**
**Free Cash Flow = Cash from Operations - Capital Expenditures**

**Cash Flow to Debt:**
**Cash Flow to Debt = Cash from Operations ÷ Total Debt**

### Luxembourg Considerations

**Ratios Help:**
- Assess liquidity
- Evaluate solvency
- Compare performance
- Make decisions

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

Cash flow ratios in Luxembourg:
- Useful for analysis
- Help with decisions
- Compare to benchmarks
- Important for stakeholders
