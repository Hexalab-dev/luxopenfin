# Key Terms

- **Direct Method**: Shows actual cash receipts and payments
- **Financing Activities**: Cash from borrowing, equity, dividends
- **Indirect Method**: Starts with net income and adjusts
- **Investing Activities**: Cash from purchase/sale of assets
- **Operating Activities**: Cash from day-to-day operations
- **Statement of Cash Flows**: Shows cash receipts and payments
