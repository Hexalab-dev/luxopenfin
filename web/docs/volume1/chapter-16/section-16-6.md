# 16.6 Appendix: Prepare a Completed Statement of Cash Flows Using the Direct Method

### Direct Method

**Direct Method:**
- Shows actual cash receipts and payments
- More detailed
- Less common
- Shows cash from customers, cash to suppliers, etc.

**Format:**
```
Cash from Customers                  €XX,XXX
Cash to Suppliers                    (XX,XXX)
Cash to Employees                    (XX,XXX)
Cash for Expenses                    (XX,XXX)
Cash from Operating Activities       €XX,XXX
```

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

Direct method in Luxembourg:
- Less common
- More detailed
- Must follow PCN format
- Must comply with standards
