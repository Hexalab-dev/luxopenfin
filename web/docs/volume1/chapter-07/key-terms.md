# Key Terms

- **Accounting Information System (AIS)**: System that collects, processes, stores, and reports financial information
- **Cash Disbursements Journal**: Special journal recording all cash paid out
- **Cash Receipts Journal**: Special journal recording all cash received
- **eCDF**: Electronic Collection of Financial Data - Luxembourg's electronic filing platform
- **FAIA**: Fichier d'Audit Informatisé AED - Computerized audit file (XML, OECD SAF-T)
- **Fiscal Cash Register**: Certified cash register meeting legal requirements
- **General Journal**: Journal for transactions that don't fit special journals
- **Purchases Journal**: Special journal recording credit purchases
- **Sales Journal**: Special journal recording credit sales
- **Subsidiary Ledger**: Detailed ledger for individual items (customers, suppliers) that support a general ledger control account
