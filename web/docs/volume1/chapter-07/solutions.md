# Solutions


## Multiple Choice Questions - Solutions

1. **An accounting information system consists of how many main components?**
   - **Answer:** c) Five components: People, Procedures, Data, Software, IT Infrastructure.

2. **Which special journal is used to record credit sales?**
   - **Answer:** b) Sales Journal records credit sales.

3. **In Luxembourg, VAT returns must be filed through:**
   - **Answer:** c) All VAT returns must be filed electronically through eCDF.

4. **FAIA files must be in which format?**
   - **Answer:** c) FAIA must be in XML format based on OECD SAF-T standard.

5. **Which accounting software is specifically designed for Luxembourg?**
   - **Answer:** c) Sage BOB is specifically designed for Belgian and Luxembourg markets.

6. **A subsidiary ledger total should equal:**
   - **Answer:** b) Subsidiary ledger total must equal the general ledger control account.

7. **Fiscal cash registers are required for:**
   - **Answer:** c) Fiscal cash registers are required for restaurants, retail stores, and businesses with cash sales.

8. **Which component of an AIS includes the rules and methods for processing data?**
   - **Answer:** b) Procedures include the rules and methods for processing data.

9. **In Luxembourg, annual accounts must be filed with RCS:**
   - **Answer:** c) Annual accounts must be filed within 7 months of fiscal year end.

10. **Digital invoices in Luxembourg can be in which format?**
   - **Answer:** c) Digital invoices can be in PDF, XML, UBL, or Peppol formats.
