# Chapter 7 – Exercises & Cases

## Multiple Choice Questions

1. An accounting information system consists of how many main components?  
   a) Three  
   b) Four  
   c) Five  
   d) Six  
   **Answer:** c) Five components: People, Procedures, Data, Software, IT Infrastructure.

2. Which special journal is used to record credit sales?  
   a) Cash Receipts Journal  
   b) Sales Journal  
   c) Purchases Journal  
   d) Cash Disbursements Journal  
   **Answer:** b) Sales Journal records credit sales.

3. In Luxembourg, VAT returns must be filed through:  
   a) Mail  
   b) Fax  
   c) eCDF platform  
   d) In person  
   **Answer:** c) All VAT returns must be filed electronically through eCDF.

4. FAIA files must be in which format?  
   a) PDF  
   b) Excel  
   c) XML (OECD SAF-T)  
   d) Word  
   **Answer:** c) FAIA must be in XML format based on OECD SAF-T standard.

5. Which accounting software is specifically designed for Luxembourg?  
   a) QuickBooks  
   b) Xero  
   c) Sage BOB  
   d) Excel  
   **Answer:** c) Sage BOB is specifically designed for Belgian and Luxembourg markets.

6. A subsidiary ledger total should equal:  
   a) Total assets  
   b) The general ledger control account  
   c) Total revenue  
   d) Total expenses  
   **Answer:** b) Subsidiary ledger total must equal the general ledger control account.

7. Fiscal cash registers are required for:  
   a) All businesses  
   b) Only large businesses  
   c) Restaurants and retail stores  
   d) Service businesses only  
   **Answer:** c) Fiscal cash registers are required for restaurants, retail stores, and businesses with cash sales.

8. Which component of an AIS includes the rules and methods for processing data?  
   a) People  
   b) Procedures  
   c) Data  
   d) Software  
   **Answer:** b) Procedures include the rules and methods for processing data.

9. In Luxembourg, annual accounts must be filed with RCS:  
   a) Within 3 months  
   b) Within 5 months  
   c) Within 7 months  
   d) Within 12 months  
   **Answer:** c) Annual accounts must be filed within 7 months of fiscal year end.

10. Digital invoices in Luxembourg can be in which format?  
    a) PDF only  
    b) XML only  
    c) PDF, XML, UBL, Peppol  
    d) Paper only  
    **Answer:** c) Digital invoices can be in PDF, XML, UBL, or Peppol formats.

## Problems Set A

**Problem A-1: Special Journal Entries**

Record the following transactions in the appropriate special journals:

a) Credit sale of €1,000 (excluding VAT), VAT 17%, to Customer A
b) Credit purchase of €2,000 inventory (excluding VAT), VAT 17%, from Supplier X
c) Cash sale of €500 (excluding VAT), VAT 17%
d) Payment of €2,340 to Supplier X
e) Collection of €1,170 from Customer A

**Problem A-2: Subsidiary Ledger**

Create an Accounts Receivable subsidiary ledger for the following customers:

- Customer A: Beginning balance €500, Sale €1,000, Collection €800
- Customer B: Beginning balance €0, Sale €2,000, Collection €1,000
- Customer C: Beginning balance €300, Sale €500, No collections

Calculate total and verify it equals the Accounts Receivable control account balance of €2,500.

**Problem A-3: eCDF Filing**

A business needs to file its quarterly VAT return through eCDF. The business has:
- VAT Payable (17%): €3,400
- VAT Payable (3%): €300
- VAT Recoverable (17%): €2,000
- VAT Recoverable (3%): €100

Calculate net VAT and explain the eCDF filing process.

**Problem A-4: Software Selection**

List the criteria a Luxembourg SME should consider when selecting accounting software. Rank them in order of importance.

**Problem A-5: FAIA Preparation**

What data must be included in a FAIA file? List the main components and explain why each is important.

1. Credit sale €5,000 (excluding VAT), VAT 17%, to Customer A  
2. Credit purchase €8,000 inventory (excluding VAT), VAT 17%, from Supplier X  
3. Cash sale €2,000 (excluding VAT), VAT 17%  
4. Credit sale €1,000 children's clothing (excluding VAT), VAT 3%, to Customer B  
5. Payment €8,000 to Supplier X  
6. Collection €5,850 from Customer A  
7. Pay rent €1,200  
8. Pay salaries €3,000  

Post totals to general ledger and individual items to subsidiary ledgers.

Prepare a reconciliation showing that Accounts Receivable subsidiary ledger totals equal the general ledger control account. Use the following data:

**Subsidiary Ledger:**
- Customer A: €2,500

- Customer D: €500

**General Ledger Control Account:** €5,500

Verify and explain any differences.

## Cases

**Case 7-1: Choosing the Right System**

Marie's restaurant has outgrown her manual accounting system. She's considering:
- Sage BOB (recommended by her accountant)
- Odoo (recommended by a tech-savvy friend)
- Continuing with Excel (cheapest option)

**Business Needs:**
- Track restaurant sales (service) and retail sales (merchandise)
- Handle multiple VAT rates (3%, 8%, 14%, 17%)
- File VAT returns monthly
- Prepare annual accounts
- 2-3 users
- Budget: €50-100/month
- Limited technical expertise

**Questions for Analysis:**

1. What are the advantages and disadvantages of each option for Marie's restaurant?  

2. Which system would you recommend? Why?  

3. What features are most important for Marie's specific needs?  

4. How would each system handle:  
   a) Multiple VAT rates?  
   b) eCDF filing?  
   c) FAIA export?  
   d) PCN compliance?  

5. What implementation challenges might Marie face?  

6. What training and support would Marie need?  

**Case 7-2: eCDF Filing Problems**

A Luxembourg SME is having trouble filing its VAT return through eCDF. The business:
- Uses accounting software (not Sage BOB)
- Has multiple VAT rates
- Files quarterly
- Has limited IT knowledge

**Problems:**
- Data doesn't match between system and eCDF
- VAT calculations seem incorrect
- System doesn't export in eCDF format
- Filing is rejected

**Questions for Analysis:**

1. What could be causing these problems?  

2. How should the business verify its accounting data before filing?  

3. What steps should be taken to resolve the issues?  

4. How can the business ensure accurate eCDF filing in the future?  

5. Should the business consider changing software? Why or why not?  

6. What professional help might be needed?
