# 7.6 Luxembourg Accounting Software Landscape (Sage BOB, Odoo, etc.)

### Overview of Luxembourg Accounting Software

Luxembourg businesses have several software options, each with different strengths and features. Understanding the landscape helps businesses choose the right solution.

### Sage BOB

**Overview:**
Cloud-connected accounting software specifically designed for Belgian and Luxembourg markets.

**Key Features:**
- ✅ Full PCN 2020 compliance
- ✅ Excellent VAT handling (all 4 rates)
- ✅ eCDF integration built-in
- ✅ FAIA export capability
- ✅ Multilingual (FR, DE, EN, NL)
- ✅ Strong Luxembourg localization
- ✅ Import/export via Excel/CSV
- ✅ Bank reconciliation
- ✅ Good customer support in Luxembourg

**Best For:**
- Established SMEs needing full compliance
- Businesses requiring strong Luxembourg support
- Companies with complex VAT requirements
- Businesses wanting cloud-based solution

**Pricing:**
- Starting from €30-50/month
- Varies by features and users
- Annual plans available

**Training:**
- House of Training offers courses
- Online tutorials available
- Local support network

### Odoo (Luxembourg Localization)

**Overview:**
Open-source ERP with Luxembourg-specific modules and localizations.

**Key Features:**
- ✅ Open-source core (free)
- ✅ Luxembourg localization available
- ✅ Highly customizable
- ✅ Comprehensive ERP features
- ✅ Good community support
- ✅ Can be self-hosted or cloud
- ✅ Multi-company support
- ⚠️ Requires technical knowledge for setup

**Best For:**
- Tech-savvy SMEs
- Businesses needing full ERP functionality
- Companies with IT resources
- Businesses wanting customization

**Pricing:**
- Free (community edition)
- €24.90/user/month (cloud hosted)
- Self-hosted: hosting costs only

**Training:**
- Online documentation
- Community forums
- Professional training available

### QuickBooks (with Luxembourg Setup)

**Overview:**
Popular international accounting software, requires manual Luxembourg configuration.

**Key Features:**
- ✅ User-friendly interface
- ✅ Good mobile apps
- ✅ Strong reporting
- ✅ Bank integration
- ⚠️ Not specifically designed for Luxembourg
- ⚠️ PCN compliance requires manual setup
- ⚠️ Limited Luxembourg-specific features

**Best For:**
- Small businesses with simple needs
- International companies familiar with QuickBooks
- Businesses willing to adapt software

**Pricing:**
- €15-50/month depending on plan

### Xero

**Overview:**
Cloud-based accounting software, popular internationally.

**Key Features:**
- ✅ Modern, intuitive interface
- ✅ Excellent bank reconciliation
- ✅ Good mobile apps
- ✅ Strong third-party integrations
- ⚠️ Limited Luxembourg localization
- ⚠️ PCN compliance requires setup
- ⚠️ eCDF integration not built-in

**Best For:**
- Small businesses with international operations
- Tech-forward companies
- Businesses prioritizing ease of use

**Pricing:**
- €12-35/month depending on plan

### Excel-Based Solutions

**Overview:**
Custom Excel templates or add-ins for accounting.

**Key Features:**
- ✅ Low cost (or free)
- ✅ Highly customizable
- ✅ Familiar interface
- ❌ Manual data entry
- ❌ Error-prone
- ❌ Limited automation
- ❌ No built-in compliance features

**Best For:**
- Very small businesses
- Businesses with simple transactions
- Companies with accounting expertise
- Temporary or startup phase

### Software Selection Criteria

**Essential Features:**
- PCN 2020 compliance
- VAT management (4 rates)
- eCDF integration
- FAIA export capability
- Multilingual support
- Bank reconciliation

**Important Features:**
- Payroll integration
- Inventory management
- Multi-currency support
- Cloud vs. on-premise
- Mobile access
- Reporting and analytics
- User support and training

**Luxembourg-Specific Considerations:**
- eCDF integration (critical)
- FAIA export (required)
- PCN compliance (mandatory)
- VAT rate tracking (essential)
- Multilingual support (helpful)
- Local support (valuable)

### Implementation Considerations

**Planning:**
- Assess business needs
- Evaluate software options
- Plan implementation timeline
- Allocate resources
- Train staff

**Data Migration:**
- Export from old system
- Import into new system
- Verify data accuracy
- Test thoroughly

**Training:**
- Software-specific training
- PCN training
- VAT procedures
- Reporting training

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

When selecting accounting software in Luxembourg:
- Verify PCN compliance
- Test eCDF integration
- Verify FAIA export capability
- Check VAT rate handling
- Ensure data export capabilities
- Verify support availability
- Check contract terms
