# 20.10 Luxembourg Craft/Artisan Business Costing

### Luxembourg Craft and Artisan Sector

Luxembourg has a vibrant craft and artisan sector, including:
- Custom furniture makers
- Artisan bakers and pastry chefs
- Jewelry makers
- Custom clothing and tailoring
- Artisan food producers
- Handcrafted products

### Craft Business Characteristics

**Typical Features:**
- Each product is unique or customized
- Small batch production
- High skill requirements
- Direct labor is significant
- Materials vary by product
- Overhead includes workshop costs

### Example: Custom Furniture Maker

**Job #601: Custom Dining Table**

**Direct Materials:**
- Wood (oak): €800
- Hardware (legs, screws): €150
- Finish materials: €100
- **Total: €1,050**

**Direct Labor:**
- Master craftsman: 25 hours × €45/hour = €1,125
- Apprentice: 15 hours × €20/hour = €300
- **Total: €1,425**

**Applied Overhead (40% of direct labor):**
- Workshop rent: €400
- Equipment depreciation: €100
- Utilities: €70
- **Total Applied: €570** (or 40% of €1,425)

**Total Job Cost:**
- Direct materials: €1,050
- Direct labor: €1,425
- Applied overhead: €570
- **Total: €3,045**

**Pricing:**
- Job cost: €3,045
- Desired profit margin: 40%
- Selling price: €3,045 ÷ 0.60 = €5,075
- Or: €3,045 × 1.40 = €4,263 (40% markup on cost)

### Example: Artisan Bakery - Custom Cake

**Job #701: Custom Wedding Cake**

**Direct Materials:**
- Flour, sugar, eggs: €50
- Specialty ingredients: €80
- Decorations: €120
- Packaging: €30
- **Total: €280**

**Direct Labor:**
- Master baker: 8 hours × €35/hour = €280
- Decorator: 6 hours × €25/hour = €150
- **Total: €430**

**Applied Overhead (35% of direct labor):**
- Kitchen allocation: €150.50

**Total Job Cost:**
- Direct materials: €280
- Direct labor: €430
- Applied overhead: €150.50
- **Total: €860.50**

### Example: Custom Jewelry

**Job #801: Custom Engagement Ring**

**Direct Materials:**
- Gold: €1,200
- Diamond: €3,000
- Setting materials: €100
- **Total: €4,300**

**Direct Labor:**
- Master jeweler: 12 hours × €60/hour = €720
- **Total: €720**

**Applied Overhead (30% of direct labor):**
- Workshop costs: €216

**Total Job Cost:**
- Direct materials: €4,300
- Direct labor: €720
- Applied overhead: €216
- **Total: €5,236**

### Craft Business Overhead

**Typical Overhead Costs:**
- Workshop/studio rent
- Equipment and tools depreciation
- Utilities (electricity, water)
- Insurance
- Professional fees
- Marketing
- Materials storage

**Allocation:**
- Often based on direct labor hours or cost
- May use machine hours if equipment-intensive
- Should reflect actual overhead usage

### Pricing for Craft Businesses

**Considerations:**
- Job cost (materials + labor + overhead)
- Desired profit margin
- Market prices
- Competitor pricing
- Value to customer
- Skill and expertise

**Common Approaches:**
- Cost-plus pricing (cost + markup)
- Value-based pricing
- Market-based pricing
- Combination approach

### Luxembourg Compliance Note

For Luxembourg craft/artisan businesses:
- Job costing essential for pricing
- Track costs accurately
- Consider high material costs
- Labor is often significant
- Overhead includes workshop costs
- Use appropriate PCN accounts
- VAT applies to sales (usually 17%, some items 3% or 8%)
- Maintain job cost records
