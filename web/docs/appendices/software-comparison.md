# Appendix E: Luxembourg Accounting Software Comparison

## Overview

This appendix provides a comparison of accounting software solutions suitable for Luxembourg SMEs, with focus on PCN compliance, VAT handling, and Luxembourg-specific features.

## Comparison Criteria

### Essential Features
- PCN 2020 compliance
- VAT management (4 rates: 17%, 14%, 8%, 3%)
- eCDF integration
- FAIA export capability
- Multilingual support (FR, DE, EN)
- Bank reconciliation
- Invoice management

### Important Features
- Payroll integration
- Inventory management
- Multi-currency support
- Cloud vs. on-premise
- Mobile access
- Reporting and analytics
- User support and training

## Software Solutions

### 1. Sage BOB

**Overview:**
Cloud-connected accounting software specifically designed for Belgian and Luxembourg markets.

**Strengths:**
- ✅ Full PCN 2020 compliance
- ✅ Excellent VAT handling (all 4 rates)
- ✅ eCDF integration built-in
- ✅ FAIA export capability
- ✅ Multilingual (FR, DE, EN, NL)
- ✅ Strong Luxembourg localization
- ✅ Import/export via Excel/CSV
- ✅ Bank reconciliation
- ✅ Good customer support in Luxembourg

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ Higher cost than some alternatives
- ❌ Learning curve for new users
- ❌ Limited customization options

**Pricing:**
- Starting from €30-50/month (varies by features)
- Annual plans available with discounts

**Best For:**
- Established SMEs needing full compliance
- Businesses requiring strong Luxembourg support
- Companies with complex VAT requirements

**PCN Compliance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Excellent)
**VAT Management:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Excellent)
**Ease of Use:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Good)
**Support:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Excellent)

### 2. Odoo (Luxembourg Localization)

**Overview:**
Open-source ERP with Luxembourg-specific modules and localizations.

- ✅ Luxembourg localization available
- ✅ Highly customizable

- ✅ Good community support
- ✅ Can be self-hosted or cloud
- ✅ Multi-company support

- ❌ Luxembourg localization may need configuration

**Best For:**
- Tech-savvy SMEs
- Businesses needing full ERP functionality
- Companies with IT resources

**PCN Compliance:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Good, with setup)

**Ease of Use:** ⭐⭐⭐ (Good, but complex)

## Selection Guide

### For Restaurants/Hospitality
**Recommended:** Sage BOB
- Best VAT handling for restaurant rates (3% food/non-alcoholic drinks, 17% alcoholic drinks)
- Payment method reconciliation (Cetrel)
- POS integration options

### For Retail
**Recommended:** Sage BOB or Odoo
- Inventory management important
- VAT compliance critical
- Multi-location support (Odoo)

### For Professional Services
**Recommended:** QuickBooks, Xero, or Sage BOB
- Time tracking integration
- Project accounting
- Simpler needs may prefer QuickBooks/Xero

### For E-Commerce
**Recommended:** Odoo or Xero
- E-commerce platform integration
- Multi-currency support
- Automated reconciliation

### For Very Small Businesses
**Recommended:** CashCtrl or Excel
- Low cost or free
- Simple needs
- Can upgrade later

### For Growing SMEs
**Recommended:** Sage BOB or Odoo
- Scalability
- Full compliance
- Professional features
