# Appendix A: Luxembourg PCN 2020 Chart of Accounts

## Overview

The **Plan Comptable Normalisé (PCN 2020)** is the standard chart of accounts laid down by the *Règlement grand-ducal du 12 septembre 2019*, applicable to financial years starting on or after 1 January 2020. Companies keeping Luxembourg GAAP accounts file the balance of every PCN account each year on **eCDF** together with their balance sheet and profit and loss account. Sole traders and partnerships (SNC/SCS) with annual turnover up to EUR 100,000 excluding VAT are exempt.

This appendix lists the **main accounts** used by small and medium-sized businesses and throughout this textbook. It is a teaching extract, not the full chart: the official PCN contains several hundred accounts. Always check the official text for an account not listed here.

## How PCN numbering works

- Account numbers have **no fixed length**. The first digit is the **class**, two digits are a **group**, and each further digit makes the account more specific: 4 → 46 → 461 → 4614 → 46141 → **461411** (output VAT).
- *Comptes de regroupement* only aggregate; *comptes d'imputation* (the most detailed official accounts) are where you post.
- You may open **your own sub-accounts below** an official account (e.g., 40111, 40112 for individual customers under 4011), never beside it. For eCDF, sub-accounts roll up into the official account they start with.
- **Maturity is part of the number** for many debts and receivables: …1 = due within one year, …2 = due after more than one year (e.g., bank loans 1941 / 1942).
- Since the 2015 reform there are **no extraordinary items**: disposals, penalties and one-offs are operating (64 / 74) or financial (65 / 75).

## Conventions used in this textbook

- **"Cash"** in journal entries means money in the business bank account: **5131**. Notes and coins are **516**.
- **"VAT Payable"** at the time of a sale is output VAT **461411**; **"VAT Recoverable"** is input VAT **421611**. At the end of each VAT period both are cleared to **461412** (VAT payable to the AED) or **421612** (VAT credit).
- **"Equipment"** is booked to **222** (plant and machinery); vehicles **2232**, furniture **2234**, computers **2235**.
- **Accumulated depreciation** has no separate class in the PCN: this book opens a sub-account ending in 9 under the asset (e.g., **2229** under 222, **22329** under 2232, **22139** under 2213, **2129** under 212).
- **Partners' capital** in a partnership: sub-accounts **1041, 1042, 1043** under 104; owner's drawings **10611**.
- **"Cost of Goods Sold"** has no PCN account of its own: under the PCN, cost of sales = purchases (**6061**) ± change in stocks (**607**). Where this book uses a perpetual inventory system it books COGS to **607**.
- **"Income Summary"** is not a PCN account: closing entries go directly to **142** (result for the financial year), which is then allocated to **141**, **131** and dividends after the general meeting.
- **Manufacturing overhead (MOH)** in the cost-accounting chapters is an analytical clearing account kept outside the PCN; in the financial accounts these costs stay in their nature accounts (60–63).

## Typical accounts by business type

- **Restaurant / hospitality**: Sales 703 (meals and services) or 7061 (goods); stock of food and drinks 361; bank 5131, till 516; card settlements in transit 517; VAT 461411 / 421611
- **Retail**: Sales 7061; merchandise 361; purchases 6061; change in stocks 607; VAT 461411 / 421611
- **Professional services**: Fees billed 703; professional fees paid 6134; salaries 6211; employer social security 623; VAT 461411 / 421611
- **Manufacturing**: Sales of products 702; raw materials 301 / purchases 601; work in progress 311; finished goods 321; change in stocks of products 71
