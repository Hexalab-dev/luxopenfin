# Appendix B: Luxembourg VAT Rate Reference

## Overview

Luxembourg has a four-tier VAT rate structure. This appendix provides a comprehensive reference for VAT rates applicable to different goods and services.

## VAT Rates (As of 2024-2025)

### Standard Rate: 17%
- Lowest standard rate in the European Union
- Applies to most goods and services not specifically listed under other rates

### Intermediate Rate: 14%
- Specific categories of goods and services

### Reduced Rate: 8%
- Various goods and services

### Super-Reduced Rate: 3%
- Essential goods and services

## Detailed VAT Rate Classifications

### Super-Reduced Rate (3%)

**Food Products**
- Food products for human consumption (excluding alcoholic beverages)
- Water (except mineral water and aerated water)
- Animal feed
- Seeds and plants used for food production
- Pharmaceutical products
- Medical equipment for disabled persons
- Books, newspapers, magazines (physical and digital)
- Children's clothing and shoes
- Passenger transport
- **Restaurant services** (meals and non-alcoholic drinks consumed on premises)
- Accommodation (hotels and similar)
- Admission to cultural events, museums, zoos
- Social housing

### Reduced Rate (8%)

**Goods**
- Gas and electricity
- Firewood

**Services**
- Hairdressing
- Small repairs (bicycles, shoes, clothing)
- Other specifically listed services

### Intermediate Rate (14%)

**Goods**
- Certain wines (up to 13% vol.) – beer, spirits and other alcoholic beverages are **not** at 14% (they are taxed at 17%)
- Certain other specifically listed goods

**Services**
- Certain specifically listed services

### Standard Rate (17%)

**All other goods and services not specifically listed above**

Common examples:
- Beer, spirits and other alcoholic beverages (except certain wines at 14%)
- Alcoholic drinks served in restaurants and bars
- Electronics and appliances
- Clothing (except children's)
- Furniture
- Professional services (legal, consulting, accounting)
- Financial services (with specific exemptions)
- Real estate transactions (with specific rules)
- Most manufactured goods

## Sector-Specific VAT Rules

### Restaurants and Hospitality

**Food Services:**
- **3% VAT**: Food and non-alcoholic drinks consumed on premises (dine-in), and takeaway food
- **17% VAT**: Alcoholic beverages served in restaurants and bars

**Accommodation:**
- **3% VAT**: Hotel rooms and similar accommodation

### Retail

**General Rules:**
- Most retail goods: **17% VAT**
- Food products and non-alcoholic drinks: **3% VAT**
- Certain wines (up to 13% vol.): **14% VAT**
- Beer and spirits: **17% VAT**
- Children's clothing: **3% VAT**
- Books and newspapers: **3% VAT**

### Professional Services

**General Rule:**
- Most professional services: **17% VAT**
- Includes: legal, accounting, consulting, IT services

**Exceptions:**
- Medical services: **Exempt** (without right to deduct input VAT)
- Educational services: **Exempt** (with conditions)
- Financial services: **Exempt** (with specific rules)

### E-Commerce

**Digital Goods:**
- E-books: **3% VAT**
- Software downloads: **17% VAT** (unless educational)
- Online courses: **Exempt** or **17%** (depending on nature)

**Cross-Border:**
- EU B2B: Reverse charge mechanism
- EU B2C: VAT of customer's country above the EU-wide €10,000 threshold (OSS – One-Stop Shop, which replaced MOSS on 1 July 2021)
- Non-EU: Specific rules apply

## VAT Exemptions (No VAT Charged)

### Exempt with Right to Deduct Input VAT
- Exports and intra-EU B2B supplies of goods (zero-rated)

### Exempt without Right to Deduct Input VAT
- Medical services
- Educational services (under certain conditions)
- Financial services (with specific rules)
- Insurance services
- Real estate transactions (with conditions)
- Postal services
- Certain public services
- Some financial transactions

## VAT Registration Thresholds

### Standard Registration
- **Mandatory charging of VAT**: If annual turnover exceeds €50,000 (excluding VAT) since 1 January 2025
- **Before 2025**: The threshold was €35,000

### SME VAT Scheme (Franchise, art. 57bis LTVA)
- **Threshold**: €50,000 annual turnover (since 1 January 2025; €35,000 before)
- **Effect**: No VAT charged on sales, no input VAT deduction
- **Option**: Businesses may opt for normal VAT taxation

### Cash Accounting
- Optional for businesses with turnover up to €500,000

## VAT Return Filing

### Filing Frequency
Decided by annual **turnover** (excluding VAT), not by the amount of VAT owed; the AED assigns the regime in writing:
- **Annual only**: Turnover up to €112,000
- **Quarterly + annual return**: Turnover €112,000 – €620,000
- **Monthly + annual return**: Turnover above €620,000

### Filing Deadlines
- Monthly returns: By 15th of following month
- Quarterly returns: By 15th of month following quarter end
- Annual returns: Before 1 March of the following year (annual-only filers); before 1 May of the following year (monthly and quarterly filers)
- Recapitulative statements (EC sales lists): By 25th of following month

### Electronic Filing
- All VAT returns must be filed electronically via **eCDF** platform
- Paper returns no longer accepted

## Cross-Border VAT Rules

### EU Transactions

**B2B (Business to Business):**
- Reverse charge mechanism applies
- Customer accounts for VAT in their country
- Supplier issues invoice without VAT

**B2C (Business to Consumer):**
- Supplier charges VAT of customer's country
- Use the OSS (One-Stop Shop) for simplification – it replaced MOSS (Mini One Stop Shop) on 1 July 2021
- Imported consignments up to €150: IOSS (Import One-Stop Shop)
- Below the EU-wide €10,000 threshold for cross-border B2C distance sales and electronic services, Luxembourg VAT may apply

### Non-EU Transactions

**Imports:**
- VAT payable at import
- Can be recovered as input VAT (if registered)

**Exports:**
- Zero-rated (0% VAT)
- Must provide proof of export

## Common VAT Errors to Avoid

1. **Incorrect Rate Application**
   - Confusing restaurant rates (3% for food and non-alcoholic drinks vs 17% for alcoholic drinks)
   - Applying 14% to beer or spirits (only certain wines are 14%)
   - Misapplying reduced rates

2. **Missing Documentation**
   - Incomplete invoices
   - Missing VAT numbers for EU transactions

3. **Timing Issues**
   - Recording VAT in wrong period
   - Late filing penalties

4. **Input VAT Recovery**
   - Claiming VAT on non-deductible items
   - Missing deadlines for recovery

5. **Cross-Border Errors**
   - Incorrect application of reverse charge
   - OSS/IOSS registration issues

## VAT Accounting in PCN

### Accounts Used

**VAT Payable (Output VAT):**
- Account 461411: TVA à payer
- Record VAT charged on sales

**VAT Recoverable (Input VAT):**
- Account 421611: TVA récupérable
- Record VAT paid on purchases

**VAT Settlement:**
- Net amount (payable - recoverable) paid to/received from tax authorities
