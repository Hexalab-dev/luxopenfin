About Luxembourg VAT Rates

3% (Super-Reduced): Food and non-alcoholic drinks; restaurant and catering meals and non-alcoholic drinks; books and e-books, newspapers; pharmaceuticals; children's clothing and shoes; water; passenger transport; accommodation

8% (Reduced): Gas and electricity, hairdressing, small repairs (bicycles, shoes, clothing) and certain other goods and services

14% (Intermediate): Certain wines (up to 13% vol.) and certain other specified goods and services

17% (Standard): Most goods and services, including beer, spirits and all alcoholic drinks served in restaurants and bars

Filing frequency depends on annual turnover excluding VAT (the AED assigns the regime in writing): up to €112,000 annual return only; €112,000–€620,000 quarterly returns plus an annual return; above €620,000 monthly returns plus an annual return. Returns are filed electronically via eCDF.

Small-business franchise: businesses with annual turnover up to €50,000 (since 1 January 2025; €35,000 before) may charge no VAT, but then cannot deduct input VAT.

Cross-border B2C e-commerce: since 1 July 2021 the One-Stop Shop (OSS) and Import One-Stop Shop (IOSS) apply (they replaced the former MOSS).

Rates valid from 2024 (in 2023 only, the rates were temporarily 16/13/7/3%).
