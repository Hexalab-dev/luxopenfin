💡 Break-Even Analysis

Break-Even Point: The point where total revenue equals total costs (no profit, no loss)

Formula: Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ (Selling Price - Variable Cost per Unit)

Contribution Margin: Selling Price - Variable Cost per Unit (amount contributing to covering fixed costs)

Note: Use prices and costs excluding VAT (VAT collected is owed to the AED, not income). Break-even units are rounded up to whole units.
