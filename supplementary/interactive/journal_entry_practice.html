💡 Journal Entry Rules

Format: Date, Account Names/Numbers, Debit Amounts, Credit Amounts, Description

Requirement: Total Debits must equal Total Credits (Double-Entry Bookkeeping)

PCN 2020: Use official Luxembourg PCN account numbers (e.g., 5131 bank, 461411 output VAT). PCN numbers have no fixed length: each extra digit is a more specific account.

Example Problems

Scenario 1: Sale with Luxembourg VAT (17%)

Transaction: Invoice of €1,200 net plus VAT to a local customer, paid by bank transfer.

Hint: Use 5131 (bank), 7061 (sales of goods) or 703 (services), 461411 (output VAT).

Scenario 2: Monthly payroll

Transaction: Gross salaries €15,000, employee social security €1,942.50, wage tax withheld €1,800, the rest paid by bank.

Hint: Accounts 6211 (gross salaries), 4621 (CCSS), 46124 (wage tax withheld), 5131 (bank).

Scenario 3: Asset purchase with down payment

Transaction: Machine bought for €40,000 + 17% VAT. 20% (of the invoice total incl. VAT) paid immediately, balance due in 30 days.

Hint: Accounts 222 (machinery), 421611 (input VAT), 5131 (bank), 44111 (supplier).

Scenario 4: Purchase of goods with VAT

Transaction: Purchase €1,000 of goods for resale (excluding VAT), 17% input VAT recoverable, paid by bank.

Hint: Accounts 6061 (purchases of goods for resale), 421611 (input VAT), 5131 (bank).

Scenario 5: Pay rent

Transaction: Pay €800 office rent by bank transfer (rent of premises, VAT ignored).

Hint: Accounts 6111 (rent of premises), 5131 (bank).
