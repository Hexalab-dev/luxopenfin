Luxembourg Social Charges Rates (CCSS, as of 1 January 2026 — check ccss.lu each year)

Employee Portion (12.95%): Health Insurance 2.80% + 0.25% (cash benefits), Pension 8.50%, Dependency Insurance 1.40% (on gross minus 1/4 of the social minimum wage = €675.94; no ceiling). 12.95% is the headline total; the effective rate is slightly lower because of the allowance.

Employer Portion (12.57%): Health Insurance 2.80% + 0.25%, Pension 8.50%, Accident Insurance 0.65%, Health at Work 0.14%, Mutualité des employeurs 0.23% (class 1 assumed; classes 2–4: 0.95% / 1.56% / 2.66%). No employer dependency insurance.

Total Social Charges: about 25.52% of gross salary. Health, pension and employer contributions are capped at 5 × the social minimum wage (€2,703.74 × 5 = €13,518.70/month at 1 January 2026). Wage tax (withheld per the employee's tax card) is not calculated here.
