Generate Balance Sheet, Income Statement, and Cash Flow Statement from Trial Balance

💡 How to Use

1. Enter company information and period dates

2. Input trial balance data (PCN 2020 account number, name, debit, credit balances). Accounts are classified by PCN class: 1 equity, provisions and financial debts; 2 fixed assets; 3 inventories; 4 receivables and payables; 5 bank and cash; 6 charges; 7 income.

3. Click "Generate Statements" to create financial statements

4. Export to Excel or print for your records
