About PCN 2020

The Plan Comptable Normalisé (PCN 2020), laid down by the Règlement grand-ducal of 12 September 2019, is the standard chart of accounts for Luxembourg GAAP bookkeeping (sole traders and partnerships with turnover up to EUR 100,000 excl. VAT are exempt). Accounts are organised into 7 classes. Numbers have no fixed length: the first digit is the class, two digits a group, and each further digit is more specific (4 → 46 → 461 → 4614 → 46141 → 461411 output VAT). You may open your own sub-accounts below an official account. This tool lists selected accounts used in the textbook, not the full chart.
