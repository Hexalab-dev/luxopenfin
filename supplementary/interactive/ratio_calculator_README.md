# Financial Ratio Calculator Tool - User Guide

## Overview

The Financial Ratio Calculator is an interactive web-based tool that calculates and analyzes financial ratios from financial statement data. It provides comprehensive ratio analysis including liquidity, profitability, efficiency, and solvency ratios with automatic interpretation.

## Features

### ✅ Core Functionality
- **Liquidity Ratios**: Current ratio, quick ratio, working capital
- **Profitability Ratios**: Gross margin, net margin, ROA, ROE
- **Efficiency Ratios**: Inventory turnover, receivables turnover, DSO
- **Solvency Ratios**: Debt to equity, debt to assets
- **Automatic Interpretation**: Status indicators and explanations
- **Benchmark Comparison**: Industry-standard benchmarks
- **Example Data**: Pre-loaded example for practice

### 🎨 User Interface
- **Dark Mode Ready**: Modern dark theme
- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Visual Status Indicators**: Color-coded ratio status (Good/Acceptable/Poor)
- **Organized Sections**: Ratios grouped by category

## Usage Instructions

### Step 1: Enter Financial Data

#### Balance Sheet Data
- **Cash**: Cash and cash equivalents
- **Accounts Receivable**: Trade receivables
- **Inventory**: Current inventory value
- **Total Current Assets**: Sum of all current assets
- **Total Assets**: Total of all assets
- **Current Liabilities**: Short-term obligations
- **Total Liabilities**: All liabilities
- **Total Equity**: Owner's equity

#### Income Statement Data
- **Revenue (Sales)**: Total revenue for the period
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**: Direct costs of sales
- **Net Income**: Profit after all expenses

#### Additional Data (Optional)
- **Average Inventory**: Average of beginning and ending inventory
- **Average Accounts Receivable**: Average of beginning and ending receivables

**Note**: If averages are not provided, the tool uses the current balance sheet values.

### Step 2: Calculate Ratios

Click "Calculate Ratios" to see all calculated ratios organized by category.

### Step 3: Review Results

Each ratio displays:
- **Ratio Name**: Name of the ratio
- **Value**: Calculated ratio value
- **Formula**: Formula used for calculation
- **Status**: Good/Acceptable/Poor indicator
- **Interpretation**: Explanation of what the ratio means

## Financial Ratios Explained

### Liquidity Ratios

#### Current Ratio
**Formula**: Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities

**Interpretation**:
- **> 1.5**: Good liquidity
- **1.0 - 1.5**: Acceptable liquidity
- **< 1.0**: Poor liquidity

**Purpose**: Measures ability to pay short-term obligations

#### Quick Ratio (Acid-Test)
**Formula**: (Current Assets - Inventory) ÷ Current Liabilities

**Interpretation**:
- **> 1.0**: Good quick liquidity
- **0.8 - 1.0**: Acceptable
- **< 0.8**: Poor quick liquidity

**Purpose**: Measures ability to pay obligations without selling inventory

#### Working Capital
**Formula**: Current Assets - Current Liabilities

**Interpretation**:
- **Positive**: Good liquidity position
- **Negative**: Liquidity concerns

**Purpose**: Amount of current assets available after paying current liabilities

### Profitability Ratios

#### Gross Profit Margin
**Formula**: (Revenue - COGS) ÷ Revenue × 100

**Interpretation**:
- **> 50%**: Good margin
- **30 - 50%**: Acceptable
- **< 30%**: Low margin

**Purpose**: Percentage of revenue remaining after direct costs

#### Net Profit Margin
**Formula**: Net Income ÷ Revenue × 100

**Interpretation**:
- **> 10%**: Good profitability
- **5 - 10%**: Acceptable
- **< 5%**: Low profitability

**Purpose**: Percentage of revenue remaining as profit

#### Return on Assets (ROA)
**Formula**: Net Income ÷ Total Assets × 100

**Interpretation**:
- **> 10%**: Good return
- **5 - 10%**: Acceptable
- **< 5%**: Low return

**Purpose**: Efficiency of asset use in generating profit

#### Return on Equity (ROE)
**Formula**: Net Income ÷ Equity × 100

**Interpretation**:
- **> 15%**: Good return
- **10 - 15%**: Acceptable
- **< 10%**: Low return

**Purpose**: Return generated for owners' investment

### Efficiency Ratios

#### Inventory Turnover
**Formula**: COGS ÷ Average Inventory

**Interpretation**: Higher is better. Shows how many times inventory is sold per year.

**Purpose**: Efficiency of inventory management

#### Days Inventory
**Formula**: 365 ÷ Inventory Turnover

**Interpretation**: Lower is better. Average days to sell inventory.

**Purpose**: How long inventory sits before being sold

#### Accounts Receivable Turnover
**Formula**: Revenue ÷ Average Accounts Receivable

**Interpretation**: Higher is better. Shows collection efficiency.

**Purpose**: How quickly receivables are collected

#### Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)
**Formula**: 365 ÷ Receivables Turnover

**Interpretation**:
- **< 30 days**: Good collection
- **30 - 45 days**: Acceptable
- **> 45 days**: Slow collection

**Purpose**: Average days to collect receivables

### Solvency Ratios

#### Debt to Equity
**Formula**: Total Liabilities ÷ Equity

**Interpretation**:
- **< 1.0**: Low debt (conservative)
- **1.0 - 2.0**: Moderate debt
- **> 2.0**: High debt (risky)

**Purpose**: Financial leverage and risk

#### Debt to Assets
**Formula**: Total Liabilities ÷ Total Assets

**Interpretation**:
- **< 0.5**: Low debt ratio
- **0.5 - 0.7**: Moderate
- **> 0.7**: High debt ratio

**Purpose**: Percentage of assets financed by debt

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Ratios Not Calculating
- **Solution**: Ensure required fields are filled
- Check that denominators are not zero
- Verify data is entered correctly

### Issue: Ratios Seem Wrong
- **Solution**: Verify financial statement data
- Check that values are in same currency
- Ensure time periods match

### Issue: Status Indicators Don't Match
- **Solution**: Status is based on general benchmarks
- Industry-specific benchmarks may differ
- Consider business context
