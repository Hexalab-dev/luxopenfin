# Multilingual Support

## Overview

This directory contains translation and localization resources for the Luxembourg SME Accounting Textbook. The textbook is available in three official languages of Luxembourg: French, German, and English.

## Languages

### French (Français)
- Primary language for many Luxembourg businesses
- Official language of government and legal system
- Complete translation of all content

### German (Deutsch)
- Widely used in Luxembourg business
- Official language
- Complete translation of all content

### English (English)
- Base language for international audience
- Used by many international companies in Luxembourg
- Original version

## Translation Guidelines

### Terminology Consistency
- Use official Luxembourg terminology
- Maintain consistency across languages
- Reference official sources (CNC, Guichet.lu)
- Create terminology database

### Legal and Regulatory Terms
- Use official translations from government sources
- Maintain original French/German for legal terms when appropriate
- Provide explanations for complex terms

### Cultural Adaptation
- Use appropriate examples for each language audience
- Adapt business scenarios to local context
- Maintain technical accuracy while adapting language

### Format Consistency
- Maintain same structure across all languages
- Keep formatting identical
- Ensure cross-references work in all languages

## Translation Process

### Quality Assurance
1. Initial translation by native speakers
2. Technical review by accounting professionals
3. Legal/regulatory review
4. Proofreading
5. Final review

### Reviewers
- Native speakers
- Accounting professionals
- Legal experts (for regulatory content)
- SMEs (for practical examples)

## Contributing Translations

### How to Contribute
1. Identify content to translate
2. Follow translation guidelines
3. Submit for review
