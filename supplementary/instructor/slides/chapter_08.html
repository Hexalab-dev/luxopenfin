8.2 Define and Explain Internal Controls and Their Purpose within an Organization
What are Internal Controls?
  • Safeguard assets
  • Ensure accurate and reliable financial records
  • Promote operational efficiency
  • Ensure compliance with laws and regulations
  • Prevent and detect fraud and errors
Purpose of Internal Controls
  • Protect cash, inventory, equipment
  • Prevent theft and misuse
  • Ensure assets are used for business purposes
  • Reduce errors in recording transactions
  • Ensure financial statements are accurate
  • Maintain reliable accounting records
8.3 Describe Internal Controls within an Organization
Internal Control Framework
Cash Controls
  • All cash receipts recorded
  • Missing receipts indicate problems
  • Sequence can be verified
  • Record cash immediately upon receipt
  • Don't allow cash to accumulate
  • Daily deposits
8.4 Define the Purpose and Use of a Petty Cash Fund, and Prepare Petty Cash Journal Entries
What is Petty Cash?
  • Pay for small expenses quickly
  • Avoid writing checks for small amounts
  • Convenience for minor purchases
  • Emergency expenses
  • Office supplies
  • Postage
Establishing a Petty Cash Fund
  • Based on typical small expenses
  • Usually €100-€500 for small businesses
  • Should last 2-4 weeks
  • One person responsible for petty cash
  • Should be trustworthy
  • Should not have access to other cash

8.7 Describe Fraud in Financial Statements and Regulatory Requirements
Fraudulent Financial Reporting
Common Schemes
  • Recording fictitious sales
  • Recording sales before they occur
  • Recording sales that don't meet criteria
  • Inflating sales amounts
  • Understating expenses
  • Capitalizing expenses that should be expensed

8.9 Luxembourg Cash Handling Regulations for SMEs
Cash Handling Regulations
Fiscal Cash Registers
  • Restaurants and cafes
  • Retail stores
  • Businesses with significant cash sales
  • Specific sectors as required
  • Must be certified/approved
  • Must record all transactions
