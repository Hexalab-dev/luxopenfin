16.1 Explain the Purpose of the Statement of Cash Flows
Purpose
  • Shows cash receipts and payments
  • Explains change in cash balance
  • Complements income statement and balance sheet
  • Shows cash from three activities
Why It's Needed
  • Revenues and expenses
  • Net income
  • But not cash flows
  • Assets, liabilities, equity
  • Cash balance
  • But not cash flows
16.2 Differentiate between Operating, Investing, and Financing Activities
Three Types of Activities
  • Day-to-day business operations
  • Cash from customers
  • Cash to suppliers and employees
  • Cash from operations
  • Cash from sales
  • Cash paid to suppliers
Luxembourg Compliance Note
  • Must follow PCN format
  • Must classify correctly
  • Must comply with standards
16.3 Prepare the Statement of Cash Flows Using the Indirect Method
Indirect Method
  • Starts with net income
  • Adjusts for non-cash items
  • Adjusts for changes in current assets/liabilities
  • Shows cash from operations
Operating Activities (Indirect Method)
  • Gain on Sale of Assets
  • Increase in Current Assets
  • Decrease in Current Liabilities
16.4 Prepare the Completed Statement of Cash Flows Using the Indirect Method
Complete Statement
Luxembourg PCN Format
  • Must follow PCN structure
  • Must use proper classifications
  • Must show all activities
  • Must comply with standards

16.6 Appendix: Prepare a Completed Statement of Cash Flows Using the Direct Method
Direct Method
  • Shows actual cash receipts and payments
  • More detailed
  • Less common
  • Shows cash from customers, cash to suppliers, etc.
Luxembourg Compliance Note
  • Less common
  • More detailed
  • Must follow PCN format
  • Must comply with standards
16.7 Luxembourg Cash Flow Statement Requirements
Requirements
  • Larger companies (based on size thresholds)
  • May be required for RCS filing
  • Useful for all businesses
  • Must follow PCN format
  • Must include all sections
  • Must be accurate
Luxembourg Compliance Note
  • Size-based requirements
  • Must follow PCN format
  • Must file with RCS if required
  • Must comply with standards
