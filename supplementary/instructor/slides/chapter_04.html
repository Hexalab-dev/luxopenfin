4.1 Explain the Concepts and Guidelines Affecting Adjusting Entries
Why Adjusting Entries Are Necessary
The Need for Adjustments
  • Time passes (depreciation, rent expiration, interest accrual)
  • Services are provided but not yet billed (accrued revenue)
  • Expenses are incurred but not yet paid (accrued expenses)
  • Cash is received before services are provided (unearned revenue)
  • Expenses are paid before they are incurred (prepaid expenses)

4.6 Luxembourg-Specific Adjustments: VAT Accruals, Social Charges, Provisions
Luxembourg-Specific Adjusting Entries
  • VAT (Value Added Tax)
  • Social charges (employee benefits)
  • Provisions (reserves for future obligations)
VAT Accruals
  • 461411: VAT Payable (Class 4)
  • 421611: VAT Recoverable (Class 4)
  • VAT Payable: €1,700
  • VAT Recoverable: €850
  • Net VAT to Pay: €850
Key Terms
Accrual Basis Accounting
Recording transactions when they occur, not when cash changes hands
Accrued Expenses
Expenses incurred but not yet paid or recorded
Accrued Revenues
Revenues earned but not yet received or recorded
Adjusted Trial Balance
Trial balance prepared after adjusting entries
Adjusting Entries
Journal entries made at period end to update accounts
Key Terms (continued)
Depreciation
Systematic allocation of asset cost over useful life
Matching Principle
Expenses recorded in same period as related revenues
Prepaid Expenses
Expenses paid in advance, recorded as assets
Provision
Estimated liability for future obligations
Revenue Recognition Principle
Revenue recorded when earned, not when cash received
Key Terms (continued)
Time Period Concept
Business activities divided into specific periods for reporting
Unearned Revenues
Cash received in advance for future services, recorded as liabilities
