# Solutions Manual - Chapter 15: Partnership Accounting

## Questions - Solutions

### Question 1: Partnership Formation Entries
- Record each partner’s contribution at fair value using PCN Class 5/2 asset accounts and Class 1 capital accounts (sub-accounts of 104, e.g., 1041 Capital – Partner A).
- Example: Partner A contributes €40k cash → Debit 5131 Cash, Credit 1041 Partner A Capital. Partner B contributes assets (cash, equipment) at agreed value.

### Question 2: Profit Allocation Methods
- Profits may be split equally, by capital ratios, or via salary/interest allowances with residual sharing. Journal entry: Debit 142 Result for the financial year (the PCN has no 'Income Summary') / Credit partner capital accounts (1041, 1042, …) per allocation.

### Question 3: Admission of New Partner
- Determine whether new partner contributes cash/assets or purchases interest. Use bonus or goodwill method to adjust capital balances so ownership percentages align. Update the partner capital sub-accounts (1041, 1042, 1043 under 104) accordingly.

### Question 4: SNC vs. SCS Differences
- SNC (general partnership) features unlimited liability for all partners; SCS (limited partnership) has at least one general (commandité) and limited (commanditaire) partner. Both register with RCS and follow PCN Class 1 equity tracking, but SCS requires separate capital accounts for general/limited partners.

### Question 5: Luxembourg Partnership Accounting Requirements
- Written partnership agreement lodged with RCS.
- PCN Class 1 capital sub-accounts per partner (1041, 1042, …) and drawings (10611) with supporting ledgers.
- Annual accounts / PCN balances via eCDF (SNC and SCS with turnover up to €100,000 excl. VAT are exempt from the PCN); VAT, social charges, and income tax compliance as applicable.

## Problems Set A - Solutions

### Problem A-1: Partnership Formation Entries
1. Partner A cash €40,000:
   - Debit 5131 Cash €40,000
   - Credit 1041 Partner A Capital €40,000
2. Partner B cash €30,000 + equipment €20,000:
   - Debit 5131 Cash €30,000
   - Debit 222 Equipment €20,000
   - Credit 1042 Partner B Capital €50,000

### Problem A-2: Profit Allocation (Equal)
- Net income €60,000 / 2 = €30,000 each.
- Entry: Debit 142 Result for the year €60,000 / Credit 1041 €30,000 / Credit 1042 €30,000.

### Problem A-3: Profit Allocation (Capital Ratio)
- Ratios 60% (Partner A) and 40% (Partner B).
- Allocation: A €30,000; B €20,000. Same entry pattern as above.

### Problem A-4: Salary Allowances + Residual
- Salary allowances = €25,000 + €20,000 = €45,000.
- Remainder = €80,000 − €45,000 = €35,000 split equally ⇒ €17,500 each.
- Total: Partner A €42,500; Partner B €37,500.
- Entry: Debit 142 €80,000 / Credit 1041 €42,500 / Credit 1042 €37,500.

### Problem A-5: Partner Withdrawal
- Partner A capital €50,000 withdrawn at book value:
  - Debit 1041 Partner A Capital €50,000
  - Credit 5131 Cash €50,000

## Problems Set B - Solutions

### Problem B-1: Complete Partnership Cycle
1. Formation: Debit 5131 €100,000 / Credit 1041 €60,000 / Credit 1042 €40,000.
2. Profit allocation: Debit 142 €100,000 / Credit 1041 €60,000 / Credit 1042 €40,000.
3. Withdrawals: Debit 10611 Drawings – A €15,000 and Debit 10611 Drawings – B €10,000 (one sub-account per partner) / Credit 5131 €25,000; at year-end close drawings to capital (Debit 1041 €15,000, Debit 1042 €10,000 / Credit 10611 €25,000).
4. Ending capital balances:
   - Partner A: 60,000 + 60,000 − 15,000 = **€105,000**
   - Partner B: 40,000 + 40,000 − 10,000 = **€70,000**

### Problem B-2: Admission with Investment (Bonus to Existing Partners)
- Capital before admission = €100,000 (A €60,000; B €40,000).
- Total capital after investment = €100,000 + €60,000 = €160,000.
- Partner C's capital = 1/3 × €160,000 = €53,333.
- Bonus to existing partners = €60,000 − €53,333 = €6,667, split 60/40: A €4,000; B €2,667.
- Entry:
  - Debit 5131 Cash €60,000
  - Credit 1041 Partner A Capital €4,000
  - Credit 1042 Partner B Capital €2,667
  - Credit 1043 Partner C Capital €53,333
- Revised capitals: A €64,000; B €42,667; C €53,333 (total €160,000; C holds one-third).

### Problem B-3: Admission via Purchase
- Partner C pays Partner A directly; partnership assets unchanged.
- Transfer capital: Debit 1041 Partner A Capital €30,000 / Credit 1043 Partner C Capital €30,000. (Premium €5,000 is personal to Partner A; not recorded.)

### Problem B-4: Partner Withdrawal with Bonus
- Partner A capital €70,000, receives €80,000 (bonus €10,000 borne by Partners B & C at 60/40 = €6,000 / €4,000).
- Entry:
  - Debit 1041 Partner A Capital €70,000
  - Debit 1042 Partner B Capital €6,000
  - Debit 1043 Partner C Capital €4,000
  - Credit 5131 Cash €80,000

### Problem B-5: Complex Profit Allocation
- Salary allowances: A €30,000; B €25,000
- Interest: A €2,500; B €1,500
- Total allowances = €59,000
- Remainder = €120,000 − €59,000 = €61,000 → split 50/50 = €30,500 each
- Final allocations: Partner A €63,000; Partner B €57,000.

## Comprehensive Problem 15 - Solutions

Accounts: Marie 1041, Jean 1042, Luc 1043 (capital sub-accounts under 104); drawings 10611 (one sub-account per partner); profit is allocated from 142 Result for the financial year.

### 1. Formation Entries (Jan 1, 2024)
- Debit 5131 Cash €80,000 / Credit 1041 Marie Capital €80,000
- Debit 5131 Cash €50,000; Debit 222 Equipment €30,000 / Credit 1042 Jean Capital €80,000

### 2. 2024 Operations
- Profit allocation (60/40): Debit 142 €120,000 / Credit 1041 €72,000 / Credit 1042 €48,000
- Withdrawals: Debit 10611 (Marie) €20,000, Debit 10611 (Jean) €15,000 / Credit 5131 €35,000; closed to capital at year-end.
- Ending 2024 capital balances:
  - Marie: 80,000 + 72,000 − 20,000 = **€132,000**
  - Jean: 80,000 + 48,000 − 15,000 = **€113,000**

### 3. 2025 Operations Before Admission
- Profit: Debit 142 €150,000 / Credit 1041 €90,000 / Credit 1042 €60,000
- Withdrawals: Marie €25,000, Jean €20,000 (Debit 10611 / Credit 5131 €45,000)
- Book capital before valuation: Marie €197,000; Jean €153,000 (total €350,000).
- The partners agree the partnership is worth **€200,000** before admission, so net assets are written down by €150,000, shared in the 60/40 profit ratio: Debit 1041 €90,000, Debit 1042 €60,000 / Credit the asset accounts concerned €150,000.
- Capital after revaluation: Marie €107,000; Jean €93,000 (total €200,000).

### 4. Admission of Luc (bonus method, as in Section 15.4)
- Total capital after investment = €200,000 + €70,000 = €270,000.
- Luc's capital = 1/3 × €270,000 = **€90,000**.
- Luc's capital exceeds his €70,000 investment, so the computed bonus of **€20,000 goes to the new partner**, charged to Marie and Jean 60/40 (Marie €12,000; Jean €8,000).
- Entry:
  - Debit 5131 Cash €70,000
  - Debit 1041 Marie Capital €12,000
  - Debit 1042 Jean Capital €8,000
  - Credit 1043 Luc Capital €90,000
- Capital after admission (Dec 31, 2025): Marie €95,000; Jean €85,000; Luc €90,000 (total €270,000) — Jean's €85,000 matches the balance given for 2026.
- Check: Luc's investment €70,000 < 1/3 of total capital €90,000, so the bonus goes to the new partner, as the exercise states (a bonus to the existing partners would require an investment above €100,000 = €200,000 ÷ 2). Entry check: 70,000 + 12,000 + 8,000 = 90,000.

### 5. 2026 Operations
- Jean withdraws on 1 January 2026 with capital €85,000 and receives €90,000; the €5,000 bonus is borne by Marie and Luc 50/50:
  - Debit 1042 Jean Capital €85,000
  - Debit 1041 Marie Capital €2,500
  - Debit 1043 Luc Capital €2,500
  - Credit 5131 Cash €90,000
- Profit €180,000 shared in equal shares by the remaining partners: Debit 142 €180,000 / Credit 1041 €90,000 / Credit 1043 €90,000. (If the instructor prefers Jean to share in 2026 profit at 1/3, his balance at withdrawal would no longer be €85,000.)

### 8. Compliance Notes
- SNC: all partners jointly and severally liable; SCS: at least one general partner (unlimited liability) and limited partners who may not manage. Both register with the RCS.
- Partnership agreement should state capital contributions, profit ratios, withdrawal terms, admission/retirement rules.
- PCN Class 1: partner capital under 104 (1041, 1042, 1043), drawings 10611; 101 (subscribed capital) is used only by companies with share capital.
- RCS filings for formation and changes of partners; PCN balances/annual accounts via eCDF unless turnover ≤ €100,000 excl. VAT; maintain supporting ledgers.

## Case Solutions

### Case 15-1: Partnership Formation Decision
1. Capital contributions should reflect asset values, expertise, and risk tolerance; consider independent valuation for non-cash assets.
2. Profit sharing could follow capital ratios, workload (salary allowances), or hybrid formulas.
3. Agreement should cover capital, decision rights, withdrawals, admission/retirement, dispute resolution, and liquidation procedures.
4. Partnership vs. corporation: partnerships simpler and flexible but expose partners to liability; corporations offer limited liability but more compliance.
5. SNC vs. SCS: SNC all partners liable; SCS allows limited partners with restricted roles. Choose based on investor involvement.
6. Disputes handled via mediation clauses, buy-sell provisions, and documented decision processes.

### Case 15-2: Admitting a New Partner
1. Value new interest via appraisal, capitalized earnings, or multiple of EBITDA.
2. Bonus depends on whether new partner pays more (bonus to existing) or less (bonus to new partner). Document method in agreement.
3. Admission affects ownership percentages, decision rights, and dilution; update capital accounts accordingly.
4. Tax: contributions vs. purchase-of-interest treated differently; consult Luxembourg tax adviser for registration duties.
5. Update agreement to include new profit ratios, capital requirements, non-compete clauses.
6. Legal: file amendment with RCS, update VAT registrations, ensure compliance with SNC/SCS rules.
