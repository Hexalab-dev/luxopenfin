# BTS Gestionnaire Comptable et Fiscal - Curriculum Structure

:::info Reference Material
This document provides the curriculum structure for the BTS (Brevet de Technicien Supérieur) "Gestionnaire comptable et fiscal" program. It is provided as a reference for course planning and organization, not as textbook content.
:::

## Overview

The BTS Gestionnaire comptable et fiscal is a 2-year higher education program (120 ECTS) that prepares students for careers in accounting and tax management for Luxembourg SMEs.

## Program Structure

### Total ECTS: 120
- **Semester 1**: 29 ECTS
- **Semester 2**: 31 ECTS
- **Semester 3**: 30 ECTS
- **Semester 4**: 30 ECTS (including internships)

## Module 1: Accounting (Comptabilité)

### Semester 1
- **Comptabilité de gestion 1** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)
- **Comptabilité financière 1** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)

### Semester 2
- **Analyse comptable et financière** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)
- **Comptabilité de gestion 2** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)
- **Comptabilité financière 2** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)
- **Législation comptable** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **Progiciels comptables et de gestion** (2 ECTS, 51 hours)

### Semester 3
- **Comptabilité de gestion 3** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)
- **Comptabilité financière 3** (4 ECTS, 68 hours)

## Module 2: Tax Techniques, Economics and Law

### Semester 1
- **Économie générale et actualité économique 1** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **Fiscalité des personnes physiques** (5 ECTS, 85 hours)
- **Introduction générale au droit** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)

### Semester 2
- **Droit du travail** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **Économie générale et actualité économique 2** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **Fiscalité des entreprises 1** (4 ECTS, 68 hours)

### Semester 3
- **Droit commercial** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **Économie générale et actualité économique 3** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **Fiscalité des entreprises 2** (4 ECTS, 68 hours)
- **Placements financiers** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)

## Module 3: Management Techniques and Tools

### Semester 1
- **Entrepreneuriat 1** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **Gestion stratégique et marketing** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **Mathématiques financières** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **TIC 1 : L'environnement du PC et le traitement du texte** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)

### Semester 2
- **Entrepreneuriat 2** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **TIC 2 : Le tableur** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)

### Semester 3
- **Entrepreneuriat 3** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **Statistiques** (2 ECTS, 34 hours)
- **TIC 3 : Les bases de données** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)

## Module 4: Languages

### Semester 1
- **Anglais 1** (3 ECTS, 51 hours) - Multiple levels (Advanced, Intermediate, Upper Intermediate)
- **Français 1** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)

### Semester 2
- **Anglais 2** (3 ECTS, 51 hours) - Multiple levels
- **Français 2** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)

### Semester 3
- **Anglais 3** (3 ECTS, 51 hours) - Multiple levels
- **Français 3** (3 ECTS, 51 hours)

## Module 5: Internship (Stage)

### Semester 4
- **Stage partie 1** (5 ECTS, 160 hours)
- **Stage partie 2** (20 ECTS, 400 hours)
- **Stage partie 3** (5 ECTS, 160 hours)

**Total Internship**: 30 ECTS, 720 hours

## Evaluation Methods

The program uses continuous assessment (contrôle continu) rather than semester exams:

- **Tests**: Written assessments of at least 50 minutes duration
- **Other assessments**: Regular knowledge checks, presentations, individual and group work, homework reviews, reports

The weighting of tests and coursework is indicated in each course description.

## Language of Instruction

Courses may be taught in both French and English, as indicated in course descriptions. Instructors may choose which parts of the course to teach in English.

## ECTS Credit System

1 ECTS (European Credit Transfer System) = 25-30 hours of student work, including:
- Class attendance
- Preparation and study
- Assignments and projects
- Group work

## Relationship to LuxOpenFin Textbook

This curriculum structure aligns with the content covered in:
- **Volume 1**: Financial Accounting (Comptabilité financière)
- **Volume 2**: Managerial Accounting (Comptabilité de gestion)
- **Volume 3**: Luxembourg Compliance (Législation comptable, Fiscalité)

The LuxOpenFin textbook can serve as a primary reference for the accounting modules in this curriculum.
