# Volume 2: Managerial Accounting for Luxembourg SMEs

## Overview

Volume 2 focuses on managerial accounting concepts and techniques specifically applied to Luxembourg SMEs. This volume helps business owners and managers use accounting information for decision-making, planning, and control.
